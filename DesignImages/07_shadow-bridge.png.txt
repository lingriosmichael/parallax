Image 7: Shadow Bridge perspective puzzle.

Create a gameplay concept in which the two players perceive different dimensional representations of the same space.

Player A's reality is a richly layered 2.5D ancient plaza containing enormous arches, a rotating sculpture and a powerful warm spotlight. Cat A walks around the sculpture.

Player B's reality is transformed into an extremely elegant cyan architectural blueprint: flat geometric planes, measurement lines, grids and abstract diagrams.

Cat A's shadow in the warm world becomes a solid traversable platform inside Player B's blueprint reality.

Cat B carefully walks across the moving shadow bridge while Player A positions themselves to keep the bridge aligned.

Make the causal relationship visually clear while retaining mystery and beauty.

Image 13: PARALLAX cat character design sheet.

Create a professional character-development sheet for the two playable cats.

Both cats should have elegant, simple silhouettes suitable for 2D/2.5D animation and readable at small mobile-screen sizes.

Cat A belongs visually to the warm organic reality. Use subtle design cues inspired by sandstone, fabric, natural shapes and flowing curves without turning the cat into a fantasy creature.

Cat B belongs visually to the crystalline reality. Use slightly sharper shapes, restrained geometric markings and subtle cyan accents without making the cat robotic.

Show each cat from side view, three-quarter view and front view.

Include small expressive poses:

idle, curious, frightened, jumping, pulling an object, falling sideways and celebrating.

The cats should feel intelligent and emotionally expressive but remain actual stylized cats rather than anthropomorphic humans.

Avoid clothing-heavy designs, chibi proportions, giant eyes and children's-animation aesthetics.

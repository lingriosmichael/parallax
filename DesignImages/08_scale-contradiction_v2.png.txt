Image 8: Scale contradiction. (variant 2 — labeled two-panel gameplay layout, "OBSERVER A: TINY IN A GIANT WORLD" / "OBSERVER B: GIANT IN A MINIATURE WORLD")

Create a surreal PARALLAX level based around radically different perceptions of scale.

In Reality A, Cat A has become tiny and explores an enormous tabletop-like ancient city where cups, statues, arches and broken architectural fragments feel gigantic.

In Reality B, Cat B appears normal-sized inside a vast crystalline observation chamber and can physically manipulate miniature structures representing Cat A's world.

Cat B moves a geometric tower with one paw. In Cat A's reality, that same action causes an enormous sandstone tower to slide across the landscape and become a bridge.

Emphasize enormous differences in perceived scale while maintaining the same underlying spatial puzzle.

The scene should evoke wonder rather than comedy alone.

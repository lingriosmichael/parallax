Image 12: Finale — Convergence.

Visualize the emotional final moment of PARALLAX.

Throughout the game, the two cats have existed in contradictory realities. For the first time, both realities are becoming identical.

The warm organic sandstone world and cold crystalline geometric world merge into one harmonious impossible landscape.

Golden roots grow through translucent geometric arches. Cyan lines become embedded within pale stone. Black water reflects both visual languages. Warm and cool light combine into a soft luminous dawn.

The two cats finally stand together on the same platform and can clearly see one another.

Their body language should be quiet and emotional rather than exaggerated.

Create a sense of relief, wonder and completion.

Avoid making this look like conventional fantasy heaven imagery. It should unmistakably feel like the two visual systems of PARALLAX have mathematically reconciled.

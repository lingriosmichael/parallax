Image 11: Reality Leakage / Collapse.

Create a late-game PARALLAX gameplay scene where the barrier between Reality A and Reality B is failing.

Warm sandstone architecture, roots, cloth and golden light are physically intersecting with obsidian monoliths, cyan grids, glass platforms and impossible machinery.

Some structures are half organic and half geometric. Portions of one reality appear through dimensional slices inside the other.

Both cats are visible because the worlds are beginning to overlap for the first time.

The cats should look small against enormous architecture collapsing and folding around them.

The mood is beautiful, overwhelming and slightly unsettling rather than apocalyptic or violent.

This should look substantially more visually intense than early-game scenes, showing that the game has earned its spectacle.

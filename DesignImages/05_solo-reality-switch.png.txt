Image 5: Solo Reality Switch.

Visualize PARALLAX's single-player mode.

One player controls both cats by switching between Observer A and Observer B.

Show a cinematic transition halfway through a reality switch: the warm ancient environment fractures into floating illustrated shards while the cyan geometric reality emerges underneath.

Cat A is fading into a translucent afterimage while Cat B becomes solid and controllable.

The two environments occupy exactly the same logical coordinates but have entirely different visual interpretations.

The transition should feel like the universe is changing observer rather than simply loading another level.

Use elegant distortion, geometry slicing, parallax layers and restrained particles.

Make this look like an actual gameplay transition on a landscape smartphone.

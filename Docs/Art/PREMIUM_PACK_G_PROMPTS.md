# Premium Pack G · The L040 finale vista (2026-11-03)

Part of `Docs/V_TASKS/PREMIUM_VISUAL_PLAN.md`, Phases 3 and 4. Golden Ruins ends each district on its landmark (Pack B). Obsidian Void has no landmark yet, so L040, its last level, ends on the same skyline as L031. This one painting gives it a destination.

**In game:** one far piece, behind everything and hazed like the far islands, about 14 u wide (the view is about 20 u wide). It's large behind L040's last climb. If it works there, it can also stand small and far off in L031–L039 and grow nearer each level, like the Golden Ruins landmarks. No part of it is a surface or a hazard.

**What past rounds taught us:**
- **Magenta background for Obsidian.** Its art is blue and cyan, so blue can't be keyed out. Claude Code keys the magenta out.
- **Simple shapes win.** Fine circuitry turns to noise on a phone.
- **Keep the glow small.** A glow that fills the image washes out the cat and the lasers in front of it.

**How to work:**
- Use a new chat and attach only R1 and R2.
- Check it small first: shrink it to 400 px wide. If it isn't one clear shape at that size, reject it.
- Save it to `Art_Source/Environment/ObsidianVoid/` with the exact name, then tell Claude Code.

### OG-01 · The Heart of the Void

**Save as:** `OG-01_void_heart_v1.png`, landscape 4:3, flat pure magenta background. **Attach:** R1, R2.

```text
I'm making a premium, hand-painted 2D side-view platformer for phones. Match the first attached image's painting technique, haze and colours exactly. The second attached image shows the ancient machine style of this world: use its shapes, not its layout.

Paint one colossal ancient structure floating in a dark void, seen from far away: the Heart of the Void. A huge broken ring of black obsidian machinery stands upright, cracked and missing pieces, with smaller shards and slabs floating around it in a loose orbit. Inside the ring hangs a calm, small core of cold white-cyan light. A few thin straight beams of hard light run from the core out through gaps in the ring. Below it, a long tail of broken rock and machinery trails down and fades into mist.

It is the most important place in the world, so it is grand, calm and silent, not busy. Big simple shapes that read at phone size. The glow stays small and contained in the core and the thin beams; everything else is dark blue-black stone, softly lit from the core. Strict flat front view, no perspective, no horizon line.

The outer edge is the structure itself and its floating pieces. Around it the background is one flat pure magenta (#FF00FF), edge to edge, with no glow, no stars, no mist and no shadow on the magenta. No text, no symbols, no people, no creatures, no red, no plants. Not a photo, not a 3D render, not a cartoon, no outlines.
```

**Reject if:** the glow covers more than about a tenth of it; it reads as a planet or a sun; stars or mist are painted on the magenta; it's busy at 400 px; anything in it is red.

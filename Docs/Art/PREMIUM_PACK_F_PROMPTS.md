### OF-01 · Fractured obsidian wall

**Save as:** `OF-01_wall_fractured_v1.png`, square 1:1. **Attach:** R1.

```text
Use the attached image only for its colour and darkness: a very dark blue-black stone. Do not copy its pattern of square blocks.

Paint a flat, front-on surface of ancient black volcanic glass, as if a huge wall of obsidian had cracked over thousands of years. Large irregular shards of very different sizes, some long and diagonal, some broken small, with no rows and no grid of any kind. Sharp conchoidal fracture edges catch a thin cold blue light. A few fine cracks glow very faintly with cyan. Most of the surface stays dark and quiet.

It must tile seamlessly: the left edge continues into the right edge and the top into the bottom, with nothing cut off at the edges. Even, soft light across the whole image, with no vignette and no bright corner. Strict flat front view, no perspective.

No bricks, no blocks, no tiles, no mortar lines, no symbols, no text, no plants. Not a photo, not a 3D render, not a cartoon, no outlines.
```

**Reject if:** you can see rows or a grid; it doesn't tile; the glow lines cover more than a tenth of it; it's brighter than R1.

# Premium Pack A2 · More hero walls (2026-11-03)

Part of `Docs/V_TASKS/PREMIUM_VISUAL_PLAN.md`, Phase 3. Pack A planned eight to ten hero walls, and three were painted. The High Citadel's big walls (L022, L025, L030) still show long stretches of bare masonry. The one carved guardian can't be stamped more often without looking repeated. These four give the walls more to show.

Pack A's rules hold. Here they are again so this file stands alone.

**What past rounds taught us:**
- **Don't attach saturated art.** Bright references turned everything orange.
- **Simple shapes win.** Fine cracks and rivets turn to noise on a phone.
- **Blue background.** Claude Code keys the blue out.

**How to work:**
- One new chat per image. Attach only R1 and R2.
- Paste the whole prompt block as it is. Each one is complete.
- Check it small first: shrink it to 400 px wide. If it isn't one clear shape at that size, reject it.
- Save to `Art_Source/Environment/GoldenRuins/` with the exact name, then tell Claude Code.

**References:**
- **R1:** `Docs/Art/Reference/styleframe_A_v1.png` (painting technique and light)
- **R2:** `Assets/_Game/Art/RealityA/Environment/Kit/ENV_Fill_A.png` (the wall it has to sit inside: match this stone)

**In game:** each piece sits on the wall at about 4 to 7 u wide (the view is about 20 u wide), behind the cat, and never over a trap, door or lane. No part of it is a surface or a hazard.

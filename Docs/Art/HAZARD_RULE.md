# The hazard rule · every level (2026-11-01, D-295)

**Growth (thorns, crystal) is there when you arrive and stays. Metal comes out at you.**

- Everything in a pit is growth, always.
- Everything that pops up, cycles or slides on open ground is metal.
- A laser that moves stays a laser (Obsidian).

`TheHazardRule_HoldsOnEveryRoom` checks this in every level. Generated from the layouts, so names are the elements' own.

# PAX-127 · Touch feel: frame rate and stick

**Status:** Built 2026-11-04 at the developer's request ("Patch it for me now") after the first Pixel 8a session. Decision D-322.
**Phase 1 size: None** (input tuning, no new system).

## Changes

- `App/FrameRate.cs` (new): 60 fps on phones, before the first scene loads.
- `Core/Input/VirtualStick.cs`: `ToRun` (full speed at `DefaultFullSpeedAt`, 0.25) and `Follow` (the centre trails the thumb past the ring).
- `Gameplay/Input/TouchStickCatInput.cs`: `ringRadius` 0.08 (renamed from `stickRadius` 0.12), `fullSpeedAt`, the follow on each stick move.
- `Tests/EditMode/VirtualStickTests.cs`: `ToRun`, `Follow`, and a flick back that reverses the run.

## Out of scope

The motor's numbers, the jump button's zone, arrow buttons in place of the stick, and the input system's update mode.

## Device check (the developer)

Build to the Pixel 8a. Run, stop and turn with a relaxed thumb; feel for delay against fast traps (L001's chase, the lasers); climb a vine (L011+).

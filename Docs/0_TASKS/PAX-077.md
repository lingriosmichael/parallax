## 1. Why

PAX-073's Phase 1 found that the game ticks at **50 Hz**: `ProjectSettings/TimeManager.asset` has
a fixed timestep of 0.02 s, and the tick is `ObserverSet.FixedUpdate`. D-056, D-057 and D-058 say
"at 60 Hz", and so do `LevelLayoutValidator` (`:22-23` ".1 u/tick", `DoorClearanceMargin`;
`:200-201` `/3600f`, `/60f`) and `SoloRoomsLayoutTests` (`:397-398, 435, 479, 542-544, 584, 646,
665, 684, 709-715, 897`). Every seconds-to-ticks conversion there is 20% off, and every "(0.2 s)"
in the docs is wrong.

KIT-2's arrow warning (≥ 6 ticks) and KIT-4's per-section thresholds are both counted in ticks.
The tick rate has to be right, and defined in one place, before either is built.

## 2. What to build

### 2.1 The decision (D-075)

1. **The tick stays 50 Hz (0.02 s).** Every level and trap was tuned and played at 50 Hz. Trap
   speeds are per tick (a FallingBlock at 0.3 u/tick falls at 15 u/s), so moving to 60 Hz would
   make every trap 20% faster in real time, and every level would need replaying. Rejected
   alternative: 60 Hz to match 60 Hz displays. That is a feel change and belongs to Phase H, with
   the judder evidence from §3.7.
2. **The rules stay in ticks, unchanged:** 12 ticks of timing slack (0.24 s at 50 Hz), 6 ticks of
   visible lead (0.12 s), `HoldTicks` 30 (0.6 s). No threshold changes. D-075 restates the
   seconds; the old entries are not edited.
3. **One source.** No code or test hard-codes a tick rate. Every seconds↔ticks conversion goes
   through one seam, the way `Time.timeScale` goes through `RunningState`. Phase 1 proposes it
   (§3.5).
4. **A guard.** A test pins the fixed timestep at 0.02 s, so changing it in Project Settings
   becomes a deliberate decision that turns a test red, not drift.

### 2.2 The fix

1. `LevelLayoutValidator` converts through the seam. `TriggerCoverage` too, if Phase 1 finds any
   rate assumption in it.
2. The listed `SoloRoomsLayoutTests` lines convert through the seam (pre-approved in §6, limited to
   those conversions).
3. `CLAUDE.md` states the tick rate and the seam rule, and lists the known `CatPlayerSetup`
   console line as noise.
4. `08_V1_ROADMAP.md` gets this ticket as a row before KIT-2.

## 7. D-075 (write as built)

The game ticks at 50 Hz: one tick is one `FixedUpdate` of 0.02 s, and `ObserverSet.FixedUpdate`
owns it. Rules stated in ticks keep their tick counts: 12 ticks of timing slack (0.24 s), a 6-tick
visible lead (0.12 s), `HoldTicks` 30 (0.6 s). The seconds in D-056, D-057 and D-058 ("at 60 Hz")
are superseded by these. No code or test hard-codes a tick rate: seconds↔ticks conversions go
through [seam], and a test pins the fixed timestep at 0.02 s. [Door clearance ruling.] [Death to
control: N ticks = X s.] [Presentation findings, recorded as a Phase H device risk.] Changing the
tick rate is a new decision: every trap speed is per tick, so it changes every level's feel.

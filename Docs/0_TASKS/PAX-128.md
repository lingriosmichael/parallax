# PAX-128 · Lasers fire for half as long

**Status:** Approved by the developer 2026-11-05 ("Half it. But only the firing."). Decision D-323.
**Phase 1 size: None** (constants only).
**Decisions:** D-197 (PAX-121, the aiming laser), D-276 (PAX-123, the trident), D-056 (periodic slack).

## 1. Why

On the phone, a laser's fire lasted long enough that a cat jumping over its line was caught on the way down.

## 2. The change

- Aiming lasers fire 5 ticks (were 10); L036's 4 (was 8); L031's trident 10 (was 20).
- Aim and lock are unchanged. L015, L021, L022 and L025 rest 38 (were 33), so their period stays 105.

  `L033Layout.cs`, `L036Layout.cs`

# PAX-093 · KIT-10: floors that move (ride, slide, drop-and-return, shrink, push)

**Status:** Approved. Runs after PAX-091, **before** L014–L015 are built. **Wait after Phase 1:** post the trace and stop.
**Phase 1 size: Full.** The motor gains a carry (a runtime motor change), and floors change size at runtime.
**Depends on:** PAX-091 accepted. **Decisions:** D-051/D-055 (tick-driven traps; `MovingTrap` Solid/Hazard, Periodic, Rearm), D-056 (3) (moving Solids: swept paths, crush partners, launch on stop), D-057 (6-tick lead), D-074/D-080 (coverage), D-079 (routes), D-088 (`ApplyLaunch`: the only other outside write to the cat's velocity), D-091 (rewind). New **D-095**, written as built.

## 1. Why

The developer (2026-09-27): none of the levels has moving platforms, floors that fall and come back, floors that slide sideways, or floors that shrink. These are Level Devil's core moves and PARALLAX doesn't have them yet. The kit has half of it (`MovingTrap` Solid moves, carries a cat **up**, can be Periodic, and Rearm snaps back), but a cat standing on a sideways-moving solid is **not carried**, and nothing shrinks.

## 2. What to build

1. **Riding (the motor carry).** A grounded cat standing on a moving Solid moves with it: each tick, after the motor step, the cat's position is offset by the platform's displacement for that tick (the pure `pose(t) − pose(t−1)`), in the room step, deterministically. It applies in every direction. Upward keeps D-056 (3)'s launch-on-stop rule; downward keeps the cat grounded. It isn't applied while climbing (the vine holds the cat) or during the death hold. Jumping off a moving platform adds its horizontal velocity to the jump (one rule, stated in D-095).
2. **Five floor patterns** (builder + layout data on the existing `MovingTrap`, new code only where a pattern needs it):
   - **Mover:** a platform on a back-and-forth path (Periodic, horizontal or vertical); ridden.
   - **Slide-away:** on a trigger, the floor slides sideways out from under the cat. Per element: **Carry** (you ride it into danger) or **Slip** (it slides out from under you and you drop). Slip needs the carry to be switched off for that element.
   - **Drop-and-return:** the floor drops (falls like a block, harmless to stand on until it's gone), then after a cooldown **rises back** with a visible return, not a snap (a new return motion on the collapse/Rearm path).
   - **Shrinker:** a platform whose width shrinks over M ticks from one side, both sides or the centre, as a pure function of ticks since its trigger; its collider and look shrink together. A cat on a part that disappears falls. Rearm restores it.
   - **Push wall:** a Solid that moves sideways and shoves the cat, deterministically: the kit decides the cat's displacement (no physics shove), and a push into a named crush partner kills (D-055 (3)).
3. **Validators:** swept paths overlap no fixed geometry (D-056 (3)); a shrinker's minimum width is ≥ 0 and its trigger passes coverage; slide-away and shrinking floors are betraying surfaces for surface coverage (D-080); the coverage search learns "a mover carries you between the ends of its path" (PAX-091's approach search gets a fourth edge), and a drop is also blocked by a **vertical wall**: fixed solids spanning the drop's full height between its departure and its landing (PAX-091 finding: L012 `Orb_B`'s natural form fails only because the search drops down the Slot past Col4/F4_A/Col3 into the Nook; `Orb_B` keeps its current trigger box); `ValidateBand`: levels 1–10 may use drop-and-return and slide-away (they're classic, readable trolls); movers, shrinkers and push walls are levels 11+ unless Phase 1 argues otherwise.
4. **Routes:** the harness sees carry and shrink with no new format (the per-tick record already holds the pose). Riding windows are timed steps as usual.
5. **Rewind (D-091):** each new state restores by formula from ticks, or by value; the rewind-equality check covers every pattern.
6. **Trap Lab room 12:** one of each pattern, with a solution and a betrayal per pattern.

## 3. Phase 1 (Full, ≤ 150 lines; at most 6 questions)

1. Where the carry goes in the tick (motor step → room step → physics) so Play and the harness agree; and how it interacts with coyote/jump buffer, `ApplyLaunch`, climbing, the gravity flip and the death hold.
2. Why a cat on a sideways `MovingTrap` Solid isn't carried today (friction? kinematic `MovePosition`? the motor overwriting velocity?); prove it with a harness fixture before the fix (red first).
3. Shrinking colliders: resizing a `BoxCollider2D` per tick versus several segments switching off; which is deterministic and cheap, and how grounding sees it.
4. The push wall without physics shoves: the displacement rule and the crush test.
5. Drop-and-return: a new return motion versus Rearm's snap; what changes in `CollapsingFloorTrap`/`MovingTrap` and in their snapshots.
6. Files; any change to frozen or shared code (`CatMotor2D` is shared; the carry must leave every existing pin identical where no moving Solid carries the cat).

## 4. Rules and stop conditions

- Every existing route pin identical (no current level has a sideways-moving Solid under a standing cat; Phase 1 confirms). A moved pin is a stop.
- Deterministic: the same input gives the same outcome; no physics friction as the carry mechanism.
- Red-first tests for every pattern and for the carry.

## 5. Consequences for levels

- **L014/L015:** their sketches may add **one** moving-floor beat each where it serves the verb (for example, L015: a mover you must ride under the storm; L014: a shrinking ledge at a vine top). It must be a decision, not decoration.
- **Half B (L016–L020):** each concept is revisited to use moving floors where they fit. L020's machine should use at least two patterns.
- **Levels 21+:** a theme can be built around them (Level Devil's "platforms" door).
- **Levels 1–10:** unchanged unless the developer asks; drop-and-return and slide-away are allowed there if they are.

## 6. YOU: Unity Editor

Run the Trap Lab menu and save; play room 12: ride a mover, get dropped by a slip floor, stand on a drop-and-return floor, watch a shrinker shrink under you, get pushed into a pit. Say whether each feels right. Then Run All (or the batch-mode command).

  a push path must end against its named crush partner or in open space; any other fixed solid in the push path is an

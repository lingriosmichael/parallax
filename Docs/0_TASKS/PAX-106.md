## 1. Why

The developer played PAX-105: jumping and pressing Grab at the same moment is too hard on a phone. Latching becomes
automatic, and since only walls he places can be held, the player needs to see them: grip faces are mossy, and nothing
else is. He also found every platform and wall side ruler-straight; they become broken stone.

## 2. What to build

### Part A · Automatic latch (D-110 amendment 4)

1. `WallClingState`/`CatWallCling`: an airborne, not-rising, gravity-down cat touching a still grip face latches on its
   own. Remove the Grab buffer and latch mode; every rule they gated now applies on every grip face. Keep everything
   else exactly as built (face lock, slide, wall jump, move lock and carry, releases, still check, top landing).
2. **Remove the Grab button:** `CatCommand.GrabPressed`, the touch Grab zone (and its reserved region and visual),
   Left Shift in `KeyboardCatInput`, router merge, seated filter, pause/hold handling, and `GrabInputTests`. Removing a
   `CatCommand` field must not change any other input path.
3. **Routes:** remove `Grab()` and `LatchMode()` from `Route.cs`; re-author L001's solution and Trap Lab room 14's routes
   without them. Room 14's plain wall now proves "touching a plain wall never latches".
4. **Reach model and validators:** they already treat latch mode as on; check nothing assumed a Grab tap, and that
   `ValidateWallJumpShortcuts`, `ValidateGripWall` and the static rules give the same results as before on every level.

### Part B · Moss means grip (D-110 amendment 4 (2))

5. A grip face (both sides of a `GripWall` the cat can reach, and a grip falling block's faces) wears a **moss carpet**
   over at least 80% of its height, brighter green than the stone, irregular at its edges (not a flat rectangle).
   Drawn by `GripSurface`/the skin; never changes the collider. Readable at phone size (2400 × 1080 capture).
6. A test proves the sign both ways: every grip face's moss covers ≥ 80% of its height; no ordinary side face has moss or
   ivy below its top band (D-114 (1)).

### Part C · Broken-stone edges (D-114)

7. Every exposed side face of floors, walls, blocks, solids and moving platforms: an uneven, chipped outline within
   ±0.06 u of the collider face (both in and out), cracks, small roots, and ferns or grass at the top corners. Tops
   unchanged. Deterministic per element (the existing `Hash(name, …)` pattern), so a rebuild gives the same look.
8. **Shorten ordinary ivy and moss drapes** to the top band: at most 30% of the face's height and at most 0.6 u. No
   single long strand (it would read as a climbable vine).
9. Moving and shrinking pieces: the outline moves with its piece; a shrinking floor's edge follows what's left of it
   (D-111 (8)'s `ShownWith`).
10. **Art source:** use the existing kit (`ENV_Side`, `ENV_Fern_*`, `ENV_Moss_*`, roots in `ENV_FG_Ferns`) plus
    code-built masks for the chipped outline and the moss carpet. If Phase 1 finds a texture is needed that the kit
    can't give, say which; the developer makes environment art in ChatGPT (prompts in §12). No AutoSprite for
    environment art.

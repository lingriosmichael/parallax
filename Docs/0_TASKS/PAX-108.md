# PAX-108 · Title screen and intro

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-19 (the developer: "Lets build something now ... How about the loading menu and intro").
Built in one go while the developer was away. The interpretation below (§1) is for the developer to confirm at review.
**Phase 1 size:** Lite (this page).
**Decision:** D-157 (Proposed).
**Depends on:** PAX-053 (Build Settings, `LevelSceneLoader`), PAX-050 (progress), PAX-A15–A18 (the environment stack).
Takes the title slice of PAX-A09 §1 ahead of it; the rest of A09 is unchanged.

## 1. What it is

The game currently starts on the placeholder level list (`LevelSelect`). This ticket adds a **Title** scene that starts
the app:

1. **Intro (about 4 s, a tap skips it):** the screen fades in from black on a live Reality A backdrop (the environment
   stack the levels use, panning slowly so the parallax layers move). The cat sits idle on a mossy ledge. The
   "PARALLAX" wordmark fades in while its letter spacing closes from wide to the key-art spacing. Then a thin rule
   draws under it, followed by the tagline "Every room is lying." (Vision §1).
2. **Title menu:** a large **PLAY** button (it reads **CONTINUE** once a level has been completed) loads the first
   unlocked level not yet completed (Level 1 on a fresh install, the last unlocked level once all are done). A
   smaller **LEVELS** button opens the level list.

- `Assets/_Game/Gameplay/UI/TitleCatIdle.cs`: the cat's idle flipbook, with a look-around every few seconds.
- `Assets/_Game/Editor/Setup/TitleSetup.cs` and `TitleSetup.UI.cs`: `PARALLAX/Setup/Title Screen`, idempotent; it creates and wires `Assets/_Game/Scenes/Title.unity`.
- `Assets/_Game/Tests/EditMode/TitleIntroTimelineTests.cs` and `TitleContinueTests.cs`.

Changed:
- `Assets/_Game/Editor/Setup/BuildSceneList.cs`: Title goes first in Build Settings, ahead of LevelSelect, once its
  scene exists (until then the list is unchanged and Sync warns).
- `Assets/_Game/Tests/EditMode/BuildSceneListTests.cs`: the Title-first cases.
- `Docs/07_DECISIONS.md` (D-157), `Docs/08_V1_ROADMAP.md` (one line).

Not touched: levels, gameplay, the frozen co-op code, `_LevelTemplate`, `LevelSelect.unity`, packages, ProjectSettings
(only `EditorBuildSettings.asset`, through `BuildSceneList.Sync()`, as D-072 requires).

## 3. Rules kept

- Every load goes through `LevelSceneLoader.Load` (D-072). No `Time.timeScale` writes (D-073). The intro is
  presentation, timed with unscaled seconds; it touches no gameplay tick.
- Buttons use `Navigation.Mode.None` (D-073). A tap that skips the intro can't also press PLAY: the menu takes input
  only 0.2 s after it is fully shown.
- No new font or package: the wordmark uses the project's LiberationSans (OFL, already in `Assets/TextMesh Pro/Fonts`).
  The licensed display font is still PAX-A09 §4.

## 4. Out of scope

Audio, the level select's look (A09 §2), settings, Android back button handling, a loading bar (the levels load in
well under a second in the Editor; measured on device in PAX-037).

## 5. YOU — UNITY EDITOR

1. Focus Unity and let it compile.
2. Make sure the open scene has no unsaved changes, then run `PARALLAX/Setup/Title Screen`. It creates
   `Assets/_Game/Scenes/Title.unity`, builds it and puts it first in Build Settings.
3. Save (Cmd+S). `git status` should list `Title.unity` (new), `TitleScreenConfig.asset` (new) and
   `ProjectSettings/EditorBuildSettings.asset`.
4. Press Play in Title: the intro plays, a tap skips it, PLAY loads Level 1 (or your next level), LEVELS opens the list.

## Amendment 2 (2026-10-31): premium flair (D-280)

The developer: "Add more flair to the title. Remember I want to get money for this game. Its a premium Game. Maybe change the font, some more animation. Some more design and visual
flair." This takes PAX-A09 §1 and §4's title slice ahead of it.

- **Look:** an old-gold inscription. Cinzel for the type, Cormorant Garamond for the tagline (both SIL OFL, `Docs/Art/Licences/`). `GildedText` gilds the letters without a shader. A turning
  two-ring sigil (after the banner emblem in the key art), a flourish, a vignette, light shafts, golden motes and leaves in the corners. The buttons are one row of gold-line plates on a
  darkened ground.
- **Motion:** the letters arrive from the middle out, light crosses the word every 9 s, the buttons rise in turn, the camera pushes in, and leaving fades to black (0.35 s).
- **The signature:** every 13 s one letter of the title shudders, drops and hangs, then snaps back. "Every room is lying", and that includes the logo.
- **Changed:** `TitleIntroTimeline.cs`, `TitleScreenConfig.cs`, `TitleScreen.cs`, `LetterSpacedText.cs`, `TitleCameraDrift.cs`, `TitleSetup.cs`, `TitleSetup.UI.cs`, `EnvironmentCapture.cs` (`PrepareLights` internal),
  `TitleIntroTimelineTests.cs`, `TitleStartGameTests.cs` (the row closing up replaces LEVELS moving into START GAME's slot).
- **New:** `Gameplay/UI/GildedText.cs`, `TitleAtmosphere.cs`, `TitleButtonFx.cs`; `Editor/Art/TitleCapture.cs` (renders the title at chosen seconds, on a clone); `Tools/Art/title_ui.py`, which draws
  `Assets/_Game/Art/UI/Title/`; `Assets/_Game/Art/UI/Fonts/` (static weights cut from the variable fonts, with fonttools).
- **Editor steps:** with `Title.unity` open and saved, run `PARALLAX/Setup/Title Screen`, then save (Cmd+S). It sets the art's import settings and rebuilds the canvas.
- **Unverified:** how the motion feels (only frames were checked), the START OVER panel's look, and everything on a device.

# PAX-098 · Validator rule: clearance above walkable surfaces

**Status:** Draft 2026-09-30 (from the PAX-A08 room audit); needs the Architect's approval. Not part of PAX-A08.
**Phase 1 size: Lite.**
**Depends on:** nothing open. **Decisions:** D-052 (the collider stays 1.0 × 0.56), D-085 (band 1 rules), D-093 (band 2).

## 1. Why

No rule checks the space between a walkable surface and the solid directly above it. The PAX-A08 audit found the tightest
gap on a solution route at 0.70 u (L017 under Door_Ledge, L012 under Stub_C), only 0.14 u over the cat's 0.56 collider,
and it found them by hand-parsing the layouts. A layout edit could narrow a gap until the cat scrapes or sticks, with
nothing to catch it. The cat's art (PAX-A08, option B) also sets a visual floor: the ear tips reach about 0.85 u.

## 2. The rule (to confirm in Phase 1)

For every walkable top (a floor, a slab, a platform at rest, and undersides in gravity-up sections) and every solid that
overlaps it horizontally directly above it (below it for gravity up), with nothing in between:
- the gap is **at least the collider height plus a margin** (passable), or
- **at most the collider height minus a margin** (deliberately impassable),

and never in the band between. Phase 1 settles:
- the two margins (a starting point: passable ≥ 0.66 u, impassable ≤ 0.46 u);
- whether a passable gap on a solution route must also clear the art (ear tips about 0.85 u, so ≥ 0.70 u is accepted,
  per the developer's ruling in PAX-A08);
- how moving solids are treated (at rest, and at their end pose);
- exemptions: a gap that exists only inside a trap's reveal, a hazard's own box, a declared crusher.

## 3. Tests (red first)

- The rule rejects a fixture with a gap inside the forbidden band, and accepts one just outside it on each side.
- L001–L020 and the Trap Lab rooms pass, or each failure is listed for the developer.

## 4. Allowed files

`Assets/_Game/Editor/Setup/LevelLayoutValidator*.cs` (a new partial for the rule), its tests. Layout changes, if any
level fails, are separate tickets.

## 5. Out of scope

Changing any level; the collider and motor; the art; PAX-097's lift margin.

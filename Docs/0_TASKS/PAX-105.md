## 1. Why

The developer wants the cat to jump at a wall, hang on with its claws, slide down slowly, and jump off the wall to reach
higher places, "like a form of double jump". Rulings (D-110): automatic grab, every solid face ≥ 1 u, two facing walls
to gain height, push away releases, gravity down only, and the 20 existing levels fixed here so the new move can't skip
their trolls.

## 2. What to build

1. **`WallClingState` (Core, pure)**, modelled on `ClimbState`: cling, slide cap, release causes, the face lock (D-110
   (5)), wall coyote, move lock and the wall-jump velocity. A face is identified by (collider instance, side ±1). All
   timing in motor steps.
2. **`CatWallCling` (Gameplay, plain class owned by `CatMotor2D`, not a component)**, like `CatClimber`:
   - **Detect:** each step, airborne and gravity down only, a horizontal `body.Cast` both ways (own-reality mask, no
     triggers, `WallProbeDistance` default 0.05). A hit counts when its normal is within `WallNormalThreshold` of
     horizontal, its collider's vertical extent at the contact is ≥ `MinClingFaceHeight` (1.0), the collider isn't a
     hazard, fake platform, trigger or vine, and the solid isn't moving this tick (Phase 1 Q3 names how each moving kind
     tells you).
   - **Order in `Step`:** after the ground test and after the climber (a climb in progress or a grab wins), before
     `JumpWindows`. With no wall touched and no cling in progress, it returns having touched nothing, so the ordinary
     path runs exactly as before.
   - **Clinging:** velocity along the wall from normal gravity, with the fall speed capped at `WallSlideSpeed`; Move
     toward the face ignored; push away (|Move| ≥ 0.5) releases and opens `WallCoyoteTicks`.
   - **Wall jump:** D-110 (4). A buffered jump on the cling tick wall-jumps (as a vine grab leaps).
   - **Releases** per D-110 (6): `ApplyLaunch`, `ApplyPush`, `ApplyCarry`, a gravity flip and `ResetMotion` all end a
     cling (`ResetMotion` also clears the lock); `Freeze` holds it.
   - Expose `IsClinging`, `ClingSide` (−1 left wall, +1 right) and `WallJumpedThisStep` for presentation and routes.
3. **Tunables** in `CatMotorConfig` as field initializers (the `.asset` is not edited; Unity fills the new fields with
   these defaults): `WallSlideSpeed` 2, `WallJumpSideSpeed` 6, `WallJumpMoveLockTicks` 8, `WallCoyoteTicks` 5,
   `MinClingFaceHeight` 1.0, `WallProbeDistance` 0.05, `WallNormalThreshold` 0.9 (|normal.x|), `WallReleaseThreshold`
   0.5.
4. **Animation** (D-036: code-driven flipbook, no Animator, no `.anim`). Appended `CatAnimState` values, in this order:
   `WallCling`, `WallSlide`, `WallJump`.
   - `WallCling`: clinging and not falling faster than `ClimbStillSpeed`. One `CatA_Hang` frame.
   - `WallSlide`: clinging and falling. `CatA_Climb` stepped **backwards** with frames advanced by the distance slid
     (as the vine climb advances by distance climbed), so the paws scrape down at the slide speed.
   - `WallJump`: `CatA_Leap` from frame 1 (frame 0 has vine residue, NEEDED_ASSETS), facing away from the wall, then
     the ordinary air rows. Landing ends it like any air state.
   - **Pose:** reuse `ClimbPose` (vertical, head up) mirrored so the paws face the wall, then shift x so the paw edge
     touches the face. Measure the paw edge per frame from the sprite's alpha (an Editor measurement baked into
     `CatVisualConfig` as a per-clip value, or computed at import by `CatSpriteImporter`; Phase 1 Q5 picks one). Facing
     stays presentation-owned (D-034).
   - **Art check (an EditMode test):** across every `WallCling`/`WallSlide`/`WallJump` frame, with gravity down, both
     sides: the drawn paws are ≤ 0.03 u from the face, no body pixel is drawn more than 0.02 u inside the wall, and no
     frame shows vine residue (`CatAnimationCheckTests`' existing measures). Capture both sides in
     `CatCaptureClimbScenarios` (or a sibling `CatCaptureWallScenarios`) for the developer's contact sheet.
   - **Assessment (the developer asked for it):** the existing art fits. `CatA_Hang` is a side-on vertical pose with the
     forepaws reaching to one side, which is exactly a cat on a wall once it's mirrored and offset; `CatA_Climb` reversed
     reads as claws scraping down; `CatA_Leap` is a push-off. **If the art check fails and offsets can't fix it, stop
     and report** with the contact sheet. The fallback is a new AutoSprite sheet (prompt in §12), which needs the
     developer's go-ahead for the spend (20 credits per asset, CLAUDE.md).
5. **Routes.** `RouteRecord` gains `IsClinging` and `ClingSide`; new conditions `Clinging()`, `ClingingLeft()`,
   `ClingingRight()`. A wall jump is `Jump()` while clinging. No other format change.
6. **Reach model and validator.**
   - `WallJumpReach` (Editor, pure): from the motor's numbers, the wall jump's arc, the **crossing reach** (the widest
     gap between two facing walls where a wall jump still meets the far wall while rising or within the slide), and the
     height gained per hop. Measured in Phase 2 through the route harness, not by hand.
   - `LevelLayoutValidator.ValidateWallJumpShortcuts` (new partial file): for every level, the clingable faces (D-110
     (2)) from layout data, grouped into **shafts** (two facing faces within crossing reach) and **single kick-off
     faces**. The cat reaches a face if any point of it is inside the existing reach envelope from a surface it can stand
     on, or from another reachable face. From each reachable face, the wall-jump arc (and, in a shaft, the climb up to
     the shaft's top) gives new reachable points. Flag any of: (a) the door footprint; (b) a surface beyond a
     betrayal's trigger reached without crossing the trigger (D-074's cut, now with wall approaches); (c) a `BaitGap`
     target (D-083); (d) a trap floor's underside that band 1 proves out of reach (D-085); (e) any standable surface
     not on the solution route that skips a checkpoint section gate (D-091). Output a per-level table.
   - Existing static checks that assume no wall jump (Phase 1 Q4 lists them all) take wall reach into account, or say in
     their failure message why it can't apply (gravity-up checks are unaffected: no cling with gravity up).
7. **Trap Lab room 13** (or the next free number): a two-wall shaft to climb, a single wall that can't be climbed (the
   face lock), a 0.5 u platform edge that can't be clung to, and a falling block that releases a clinging cat when it
   moves. Solution + one Dies (wall jump into a hidden spike, lead ≥ 6) + one Recovers.
8. **L001–L020 audit and fixes** (§5.3).

## 3. Phase 1 (Full, ≤ 150 lines, at most 6 questions)

5. **Presentation:** paw-edge offset (config value vs importer), the backward Climb stepping, and whether `ClimbPose`
   can be reused without changing the vine's pose (the vine pose must not move by a pixel).

   routes before and after with the cling switched on behind a test-only flag, then compare), without fixing anything

  open-air pin is a stop condition.
- Gravity up never clings (test it). Climbing a vine wins over clinging (test it).
- No Unity package installs, no Animator, no `.anim`, no new VFX system. `CatMotorConfig.asset` is not hand-edited.
- **If more than 8 of the 20 levels need geometry changes, or any level needs its betrayal order or solution changed,
  stop and report** with the list and options; don't redesign levels on your own.
- If the art check fails after offsets, stop and report (§2.4).
- Fixes keep every level's existing rules green: band 1 (D-085), precision (D-083), reveal leads, camera tell, door
  rules (D-109), checkpoint sections (D-091), hazard contrast (D-108), trap art reveal frames.

## 13. Rulings after Phase 1 (the developer, 2026-10-04) — these override §2–§6 where they differ

- **P1 Grab button and latch mode (D-110 amendment).** No automatic cling. `CatCommand.GrabPressed` (edge); touch
  button on the right beside jump; keyboard Left Shift; router, seated filter, pause and death hold treat it like jump.
  A Grab tap latches an airborne, not-rising, gravity-down cat touching a clingable face, and is buffered for
  `GrabBufferTicks` (6). Latch mode then auto-latches the next face reached while not rising (face lock still applies)
  and ends on landing, push-away, sliding off a face's end, running out of walls after a wall jump (state the exact test
  in your output), flip, launch, push, moving face, death, reset, respawn. `WallCoyoteTicks` is dropped. During the move
  lock, a wall jump with no Move away keeps its sideways speed.
- **P2 Not rising.** No latch while rising; a cat rising along a wall latches at the top of its rise.
- **P3 Pit walls stay grabbable.** The audit treats a Grab save out of a pit as a possible shortcut. The 8-level stop
  stays.
- **P4 Route drift:** no existing route presses Grab, so **every existing route result and tick record must replay
  identically**. Your before/after dump (Q6, no motor flag) is the proof; any difference is a stop condition.
- **P5 Approved as proposed:** a new full-suite baseline on the clone now; the six allowed-file additions; the Leap
  push-off offset eased to 0 over the clip (art check on the push-off frame only); residue judged by eye on
  `CatCaptureWallScenarios` (no automated measure); the moving-solid "bounds changed → release, one-tick still check for
  bodied solids" rule.
- **P6 More allowed files for the button:** `CatCommand.cs`, `TouchStickCatInput.cs`, `KeyboardCatInput.cs`,
  `CatInputRouter.cs`, `LocalHumanDriver.cs`, the touch button's visual/setup files (name them in a short Phase 1
  addendum; same treatment as the jump button), and their tests. Routes gain `Grab()` (one-tick press) and
  `LatchMode()`.

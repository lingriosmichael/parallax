# PAX-110 · The cat's voice: reel S (D-165)

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-22 (the developer, on round 3's reels: "Lets go with Audio S"). Built the same day on a batch-mode
clone; the developer's menu on main is pending.
**Phase 1 size:** Lite (generated sounds, one data table in Core, a layer in `GameAudio`, one idle hook, an import menu).
**Decisions:** D-158 and its amendment 1 (synthesized sound), D-165 (this ticket).

## 1. Why

The developer, 2026-10-21: "there were no cat sounds for a cat game. the death sound should also sound like a cat, but no so
obvious." Reels A, B and C (2026-10-21) were "too cartoony" and had no audible meow. Round 3 offered R (processed CC0
recordings) and S (a code resynthesis fitted to them), and they picked S.

## 2. What to build

1. **`Tools/Audio/cat_voice.py`** (new) renders the voice from `Tools/Audio/cat_voice_presets.json` (the fitted tables: no
   recorded audio) into `Assets/_Game/Audio/CatVoice/{Role}_{NN}.wav` with `cat_voice.json`. It needs numpy and scipy only,
   and it's deterministic (`--check`). Variant 0 of each role is reel S's sound; the others are other seeds and small pitch
   steps, played in turn so repeats aren't identical.
2. **`CatVoice`** (Core, new): the roles (Mew, MewLow, Mrrow, Chirp, Mrp, Mrf, Trill, Purr, Death) and, per cue, which
   voice plays, when and how loud:
   - **Respawn:** a mew over the soft pluck.
   - **Level complete:** a "mrrow" and a purr under the chime.
   - **Jumps:** a chirp or a "mrp" on every fourth jump, alternating.
   - **Wall jump:** a "mrp".
   - **Hard landing:** a low "mrf".
   - **Gravity flip:** a chirp.
   - **Inverter:** a trill.
   - **Every death:** reel S's thud with the muffled yelp. The cause's own accent (steel, slam, crackle, thunk, whoosh) stays
     synthesized under it.
   - **Idle (new cue, appended):** a soft low meow after the cat stands still for 6 s, then every 18 s while it stays.
3. **`SfxPresets`:** the formant "voice" layers (the cartoony ones) leave every cue; what's left is each cue's accent.
4. **`AudioCueBank`:** a cat-voice list (role → variant clips), written by the import menu.
5. **`GameAudio`:** when a cue starts, its voice layers start with it, on other voices of the pool, delayed, panned and faded
   with the cue. A voice-only cue (Idle) needs no synthesized clip.
6. **`LevelAudioEvents`:** counts the ticks the solo cat stands still (grounded, not moving) and plays Idle.
7. **`PARALLAX/Setup/Cat Voice (PAX-110)`** (new, `CatVoiceImport`): sets the clips' import settings (mono, decompressed on
   load) and fills the bank. Idempotent. `PARALLAX/Setup/Audio (PAX-064)` also runs it.

## 3. Allowed files

- `Tools/Audio/cat_voice.py`, `Tools/Audio/cat_voice_presets.json` (new)
- `Assets/_Game/Audio/CatVoice/**` (new, generated; Unity writes the `.meta` files, and the menu sets the importer settings)
- `Assets/_Game/Core/Audio/AudioCue.cs`, `Assets/_Game/Core/Audio/SfxPresets.cs`, `Assets/_Game/Core/Audio/CatVoice.cs` (new)
- `Assets/_Game/Gameplay/Audio/AudioCueBank.cs`, `GameAudio.cs`, `LevelAudioEvents.cs`
- `Assets/_Game/Editor/Setup/CatVoiceImport.cs` (new), `Assets/_Game/Editor/Setup/AudioSetup.cs`
- `Assets/_Game/Tests/EditMode/SfxSynthTests.cs`, `AudioSetupTests.cs`, `CatVoiceTests.cs` (new)
- `Docs/07_DECISIONS.md` (D-165), this ticket

## 4. Out of scope

- An in-game volume screen (the store-readiness work).
- Haptics (PAX-065).
- Changing the ambient bed or any trap's sound beyond removing its formant voice.
- Runtime synthesis of the voice in C# (a port of `cat_voice.py` is possible later; the tables are its data).

## 5. YOU — UNITY EDITOR (main)

1. Open the project and let it import `Assets/_Game/Audio/CatVoice/`.
2. `PARALLAX/Setup/Cat Voice (PAX-110)`. It saves the bank itself. No scene changes, so no rebuild is needed for sound.
3. Play any level: respawn (mew), a few jumps (every fourth chirps), a death (thud and yelp), stand still 6 s (soft meow),
   finish (mrrow and purr).
4. On the phone: the same, through the speaker.

## 6. Done when

- `cat_voice.py --check` reports no changes, and variant 0 of each role matches reel S's clip.
- Touched fixtures pass on the clone: `SfxSynthTests`, `AudioSetupTests`, `CatVoiceTests`.
- The developer has listened in the Editor and on the phone.

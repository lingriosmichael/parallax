# PAX-126 · After a respawn, the room waits for the cat's first move

**Status:** Approved by the developer 2026-11-04 ("This definitely needs to be a rule"). Decision D-321.
**Phase 1 size: Lite.**
**Depends on:** nothing open. **Decisions:** D-041, D-058 (the death hold), D-091 (checkpoint sections and rewinds).

## 1. Why

The developer, of L025: "if you respawn at a checkpoint and it is inline with a laser, it will start aiming too quickly …
if you respawn at a checkpoint, any trap or anything only starts being online after the cat moves. I noticed that in level 25
if you respawn at the first checkpoint, you could basically die infinitely."

## 2. The rule

After any respawn from a death (a room reset, or a checkpoint section's rewind), nothing in the room runs until the cat's
first move, jump or climb:
- **Frozen as in the death hold:** no `RoomLifeTick` advance, no trap or hazard step, no bounds check, no door check.
- **The cat is free:** gravity, standing and the motor work as usual.
- **The first step whose command moves (|Move| > 0.01), jumps or climbs is the room's first live tick.** It's RoomLifeTick 0
  after a reset, or the gate's tick plus one after a rewind.
- **Unchanged:** a level's start and a room's start through its door don't wait.

## 3. Files

- `Gameplay/Player/CatMotor2D.cs`: `ActedThisStep`, set by every `Step` (false while frozen). It's on the cat because every
  driver (the game's, the route harness's, the tests') steps the cat with its command.
- `Gameplay/Rooms/RoomManager.cs`: `awaitingMove`, set in `OnDied`, checked in `OnStepped` after the death hold.
  `AwaitingMove` is exposed for tests.
- `Editor/Routes/RouteValidator.cs`: the standing-at-a-checkpoint and rewind-exactness checks begin with a one-tick push of
  the stick up (`FirstMove`). It counts as a move and doesn't move the cat, so they still measure a live room.
- `Tests/EditMode/RoomDeathHoldTests.cs`: `AfterARespawn_TheRoomWaits_UntilTheCatsFirstMove`.
- Tests that respawn and expect the room to run on: `PauseTestRig.cs` (`TickActing`), `CheckpointSectionRuntimeTests.cs`,
  `GeyserRuntimeTests.cs`, `LevelCompleteScreenTests.cs`.

No frozen co-op code changes. `IObserverDriver` is unchanged.

## 4. Tests

- The new test: a level's start runs without a move; after the respawn the room's clock stays put for 20 ticks while the
  cat stands; the first move is RoomLifeTick 0; the next tick is 1.
- Fixtures that kill and respawn: `RoomDeathHoldTests`, `DeathHoldTests`, `CheckpointSection*Tests`, `MovingFloorRewindTests`,
  the trap runtime tests, `LevelCompleteScreenTests`, `PauseTickTests`, `TrapLab Room 8/11`, `ValidateRoutesTests`.

## 5. Out of scope

- Moving L025's checkpoint out of `Eye_D`'s aim zone, and a validator rule that no checkpoint sits in an aim zone. Offered
  to the developer, not approved. The wait doesn't change what a rewind restores: a laser saved part-way through its aim goes
  on from there on the first move.
- Re-recording L025's routes. They were written without a replay, and the solution dies on the descent before the Valley gate,
  so the respawn loop the developer found can't be measured by the harness yet.

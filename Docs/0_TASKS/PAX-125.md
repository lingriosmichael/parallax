# PAX-125 · Music: one melodic loop per world (D-277)

**Status:** Built 2026-10-31 on a batch clone; the developer's menus on main and first listen are pending (§6).
**Phase 1 size:** Lite (generated audio, a field on `GameAudio`, an import menu, one line in the level rebuild).
**Decisions:** D-158, D-165, D-277 (this ticket). PAX-064 §6 put music composition out of scope, so this is its own ticket.

## 1. Why

The developer, 2026-10-31: "Can we also add some sort of melodic background music that matches the background?" Until now every
scene looped the same ambient bed (wind, a held chord, birds).

## 2. What to build

1. **`Tools/Audio/music.py`** (new): the two scores, written out note by note, and additive instruments, rendered to
   `Assets/_Game/Audio/Music/{Track}.wav` with `music.json`. numpy and scipy only; deterministic (`--check`). Each loop is seamless:
   notes, reverb, echoes and noise wrap round the end.
   - **GoldenRuins** (the sunset, the waterfalls, the ivy): 6/8, G major, 83 s. Harp arpeggios, a harp then ocarina then bowed lead, a
     tiptoeing pizzicato and a chromatic slide for the mischief, running water, a few birds.
   - **ObsidianVoid** (stars, teal nebula, cold glass): 4/4 at 72, D minor, 87 s. A glass bell lead on long echoes, then a
     theremin-like voice doubled by bells, a quiet glass arpeggio, a choir pad, a soft pulse, far-off glints.
2. **`MusicTrack`** (Core, in `AudioCue.cs`): the two tracks.
3. **`AudioCueBank`:** a track → clip list and `MusicFor(track)`.
4. **`GameAudio`:** a serialized `track`. The scene's loop takes the bed's place on the bed's source, so the Music volume, fade-in
   and pause duck apply as they did; a bank without loops keeps the synthesized bed. A new scene picks the loop up where the last
   one left it, because levels are short and restarting would only ever play the opening.
5. **`MusicImport`** (Editor, new) and **`PARALLAX/Setup/Music (PAX-125)`:** stereo, streamed Vorbis at quality 0.5, background load;
   fills the bank; idempotent. `PARALLAX/Setup/Audio (PAX-064)` runs it too.
6. **`LevelSetup`:** bakes each level's `track` from `Biomes` (L031-L060 Obsidian). Title and LevelSelect keep the default, Golden.

## 3. Allowed files

- `Tools/Audio/music.py` (new)
- `Assets/_Game/Audio/Music/**` (new, generated; Unity writes the `.meta` files, and the menu sets the importer settings)
- `Assets/_Game/Core/Audio/AudioCue.cs`
- `Assets/_Game/Gameplay/Audio/AudioCueBank.cs`, `GameAudio.cs`
- `Assets/_Game/Editor/Setup/MusicImport.cs` (new), `AudioSetup.cs`, `LevelSetup.cs`
- `Assets/_Game/Tests/EditMode/MusicTests.cs` (new)
- `Docs/07_DECISIONS.md` (D-277), `Docs/08_V1_ROADMAP.md`, this ticket

## 4. Out of scope

- Adaptive music (changing with danger, deaths or a room).
- A track per level, or separate title or level-select tracks.
- An in-game volume screen, and haptics (PAX-065).
- Changing any cue, or the mix of the effects.

## 5. Rules

Audio is presentation: nothing in gameplay reads it, and no route, timing or validator result changes. No new packages. The clips
stay outside the tick: no gameplay reads `timeSamples`.

## 6. YOU — UNITY EDITOR (main)

1. Open the project and let it import `Assets/_Game/Audio/Music/`.
2. Save the open scene.
3. Run **`PARALLAX/Setup/Audio (PAX-064)`**. It fills the bank with the cat voice as well as the music, which main's bank still lacks.
4. Run **`PARALLAX/Setup/Levels/Rebuild All Levels`**.
5. Play the title, a Golden level and L031 with headphones, then on a phone:
   - the title's music carries on into a level, and on from level to level;
   - L031 changes to the Obsidian track;
   - pause ducks it;
   - the effects and meows sit clearly above it. If it's too loud or quiet, change `music` in `AudioCueBank`;
   - nothing clicks or gaps where the loop wraps (about 83 s in Golden, 87 s in Obsidian).

**Device-only, unverified:** the mix on a phone speaker, and the loop point under streamed Vorbis. If the loop gaps on a phone, set
the two clips to Compressed In Memory in `MusicImport`.

## 7. To change the music

Edit the scores or levels in `Tools/Audio/music.py`, run `python3 Tools/Audio/music.py`, and let Unity reimport.

**Depends on:** PAX-052, PAX-057 (tiers). PAX-058 not needed (novice uses no randomness).

---

## 1. Why

The first ten levels are what every player sees, and in the free set (D-062 (5), pending
D-067). They are real troll rooms from level 1 (D-053), deterministic, and must leave the
player wanting the next one.

## 2. What to build

`L001`–`L010` as `LevelLayout` data via `New Level…` / `Rebuild`, listed in order, tier Novice.
The PAX-051 seeds (old `Level_Solo01` rooms) may be kept, reordered or replaced; the table
decides.

Guidance (adjust freely): betrayals ramp from 2–3 in L001 to 4–6 in L010. Each level introduces
at most one new trap idea and reuses earlier ones as disguise. At least one gravity-flip level,
one arrow level (PAX-055), one carrying-platform level (PAX-056) by L010. Every level fits on
one screen at 20:9 (PAX-052). Rough target: a first-time player dies 3–10 times on L001–L005,
5–20 on L006–L010 (checked for real only in Phase H).

## 4. Rules

- Data only (D-054). A level that needs new trap behaviour stops here: raise a kit-gap ticket
  first, don't code it inside this one.
- Every level passes the Novice tier rules and the all-levels fixture.
- No soft-lock (D-053): every reachable state completes or dies.

## 5. Tests (EditMode)

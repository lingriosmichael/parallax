# PAX-100 · Levels 3–10: repeating and angled arrows, moving floors

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-02 (the developer: "Start with PAX-100"). PAX-099 is built and awaiting the developer's play-test and commit.
**Wait after Phase 1:** post the per-level plan and stop.
**Phase 1 size: Lite** (one plan table, no traces).
**Depends on:** PAX-099. **Decisions:** D-106 (PAX-099), D-079/D-080 (routes, betrayals), D-083 (camera tell),
D-085 (band 1 rules), D-091 (sections), D-104 (per-level camera).

## 1. Why

The developer (2026-10-02): repeating arrows at intervals that change from level to level, arrows at several angles, and
moving platforms and floors that sink or shift while you stand, "thrown around in levels 3–20, not every level needs it.
Only if it makes sense." Band 1 stays the free hook: the player laughs at a death and retries, so the new elements are
trolls you read and remember, never execution tests.

## 2. What to build

0. **First, L009's bypass (found in PAX-099, ruling 3).** A cat that runs to the post, turns, and jumps left into the
   floating Flip_D1 rises past Slab_D's left end to Roof_L at x 0.5 and walks right into the door: the room completes at
   tick 106 (the solution takes 574). It isn't caused by PAX-099's Door_Ledge. The route that shows it:
   `Hold(Right), Until(XAtLeast(6.4f)), Release(), Until(Still()), Hold(Left), Until(XAtMost(6f)), Jump(), …` (then
   `Until(GroundedOn("Roof_L")), Hold(Right), Until(RoomComplete())`). Fix the layout so it dies (a band-1 dead end, D-085),
   and declare it as "Dead end: the flip floating in front of the start, jumped into moving left".

1. **Phase 1 plan:** one row per level (L003–L010): what it gets (a repeating arrow and its interval, an angled arrow and
   its angle, a rider, a sink or a slide floor, or nothing), where, and the trap it replaces or joins. Most levels get one
   or two additions; a level whose idea doesn't fit gets none. Intervals vary between levels. Post it and stop.
2. **Build** each approved row: layout data, the solution route, a betrayal route per new element (Dies, killer, lead
   ≥ 6; or Recovers), and dead ends as band 1 requires.
3. Rebuild the levels with `Rebuild All Levels`, through its setup menu.

## 7. Phase 2 as built (2026-10-02; built and compiled, not replayed: the developer is play-testing)

- **L009, item 0:** Slab_D and Spikes_D1 (and the spikes' trigger) reach back to x 1.5 (from 4.0 and 4.25). A cat that
  jumps into Flip_D1 moving left rises at about x 3, into the spikes; it can't drift past x 1.5 before it reaches them.
  The declared route "Dead end: … jumped into moving left" stays.
- **L003:** as planned. A `Corbel` (x 20–21, y 7.5–9) hangs under S2, and `Arrow_S1` fires at −45° from its left face,
  stopping in S1_B at x 17.1. Its cut (x 18.9–19.4, the whole S1–S2 band) fires it as the cat's centre reaches about
  19.9. By the box model, a walker is hit at ticks 15–17 and a cat that stops at once is about 1.4 u clear. The solution
  and T3's "round again" wait for it (`ArrowWait`). New betrayal: T3a.
- **L005 (changed from the plan):** making Arrow_A repeat every 110 ticks is impossible. Its 31 u lane runs along S2,
  so a cat needs about 300 ticks between shots to cross it (D-056 periodic slack). A new `Arrow_Drip` does the repeating
  instead: −60°, every 110 ticks, from a `Corbel` hanging from the roof over S2 (above Arrow_B's lane), onto S2 at
  x 14.3. Its lane crosses the walk band over about 0.5 u, so the slack holds. The solution stops short of it and waits
  for a drip. New betrayal: T1b, a cat that stops under it. T2's prefix now runs through the wait. Arrow_A stays a
  single shot.
- **L006:** as planned. Pit_6 widens to x 25–29.5, past a jump's reach, and Ground_4 narrows to x 29.5–32. `Ride_6`
  (1.5 u, Carry) shuttles 3 u on a 200-tick cycle: out over 60 ticks, 40 out there, back over 60. Floor_6 and its
  RequiredJump are gone. T6 is now "a cat that runs on without waiting for Ride_6 falls into Pit_6".
- **L007:** as planned. `Tread_RC` is a sinking tread (Carry, Rearm). It goes 1.8 u down over 60 ticks, 30 ticks after
  a landing, holds 40 ticks and comes back. New Recovers route: T7, wait on it, ride down and back up, then go on. The
  solution's tail after Tread_RC is `AfterRC()`.
- **L009 (interval changed from the plan):** Arrow_4 repeats every **170** ticks on the room's clock (no trigger). The
  planned 130 would leave about 100 ticks for the crossing (up the flip, walk the roof, hop the stub), and that takes
  about 116. The solution waits on S1 short of Flip_4 for a shot to stop at the stub, then goes up. T4 is now "the next
  arrow meets a cat that stops on the roof". The flip no longer fires Arrow_4; T2's arrow is still fired by walking
  under the floating flip.
- **L010 (changed from the plan; the developer chose "slide + spike strip"):** S1 has no open air beside it to slide
  into, so the section **drops away** instead, the way Trap Lab room 12's drop works. S1_A is split into S1_A (x 0–3)
  and S1_A2 (x 6–11.5), and the ground into Ground_L and Ground_R around a new pit at x 3–6. The pit has a kill strip
  across its shaft (y −1 to −0.7) and its bottom deeper (y −4 to −3). `Drop_10` (Slip, Rearm) goes 40 ticks after a
  landing, 6.5 u down at 0.5 u per tick (faster than a fall), through the strip, and comes back after a hold. A cat
  walking across is off it in about 33 ticks. New betrayal: T7. No angled arrow in L010: angled arrows are in L003 and
  L005.
- **Lesson for PAX-101:** a repeating arrow needs a short lane where it crosses the walk (an angled or crossing arrow).
  A long lane along the walkway needs a period of about 9 × its length in ticks (D-056 slack).

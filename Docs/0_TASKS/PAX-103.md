# PAX-103 · Band 1 play-test round 2: L003's arrow floor, L004's climb, L007, L009, the storm from L005

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-03 (the developer's answers in §7); built, uncommitted (§8).
**Wait after Phase 1:** post this plan and stop.
**Phase 1 size: Lite.**
**Depends on:** PAX-102 (committed first, after the one full suite). **Decisions:** D-090 (the storm cloud; amended
here), D-083/D-085 (band 1: no precision sections, the band 1 reach and slack), D-106 (arrow angles 0/±30/±45/±60),
D-078 (lanes), D-056 (periodic slack), D-079/D-080 (routes), D-085 amendment (real holes).

## 1. Why

The developer's play-test of PAX-102 (2026-10-03):
- L003: "Put them all in the second floor, so it's a full floor where arrow traps are getting launched in different
  intervals … and one has to somehow jump and evade them. … a max of 4 arrow launchers in different intervals and
  angles." Ruling: the second floor is S2, the door's floor.
- L004: "I should be able to succeed in here if I jump across I finish the level, which shouldn't be possible." "Remove
  this inverter, instead add platforms … moving traps especially sideways, spikes in the bottom that kill you if you fall
  off the platforms. Do like 6 thin platforms, only one solution. Some platforms can move left to right." "We need to
  invert the spikes." Rulings: hard but band 1 (no precision section; D-083 unchanged); "invert the spikes" = spikes on
  the roof's underside, so an upside-down cat can't reach the door.
- L007: "spikes are just hanging in the air now? … fix this. Level 7 can be improved a lot, study the path, and fix it,
  for one it's way too easy. … from this level onwards we can start adding the storm that follows the player."
- L009: "the exit shouldn't be reachable while I am inverted. It needs inverted spikes."
- The storm: "Add for levels after 004. Not all levels need it. But I don't want to wait until level 11."
- Arrows and spikes are hard to see: a separate visual ticket (PAX-V08).

## 2. Rules for every level here

- Band 1 stays band 1: no precision sections, the band 1 reach and slack (0.75 of reach, timed windows ≥ 12), 500–1500
  ticks, dead ends that kill, every new element with its betrayal, lethal count per D-085 (≥ 5 L1–5, ≥ 6 L6–7, ≥ 7
  L8–10).
- Arrows: supported angles only; repeating arrows in one level use simple period ratios (e.g. 120 / 180 / 240) with phase
  offsets, so the pattern can be learned.
- **No door reachable upside down** (new check, §3.5).
- Exact positions, periods and phases are set in Phase 2 against the validators. Anything that won't fit is reported.

## 3. Plan

### 3.1 L003: S2 becomes the arrow floor (max 4 launchers)

- Remove the launchers off S2: Arrow_G (ground), Arrow_1 (S1) and the original Arrow_S1 (S1), with their corbels and
  betrayals. Keep Arrow_2A and Arrow_2B on S2 and add up to two more there: **4 launchers on S2, all under the roof.**
- Different intervals (from 120 / 180 / 240) and different angles (from −60°, −45°, −30°, and one from the door's side,
  +45° or +60°), phased so the walk along S2 is a run-stop-jump pattern with one way through. Each lane ends on S2's top
  (a stop face), so a landed arrow is harmless (D-078).
- "Jump and evade": if a shallow lane (−30°) lands far enough along S2 that the cat can jump over a shot in flight, the
  route uses that; otherwise every shot is dodged by stopping and going. Measured in Phase 2.
- Lethal count stays ≥ 5 (the removed arrows had 3 betrayals; the new S2 arrows each carry one).

### 3.2 L004: the climb to the door, and no upside-down finish

- Remove Flip_E (the flip under the door) and the door's retreat (Retreat, Door_Pad_L): the climb is the end, with one
  way through.
- **The climb:** six thin platforms (1 u wide, 0.5 thick) from the slab's top up to the door, two or three of them
  moving left and right (Periodic, sideways only, one rhythm). Spikes cover the slab's top under the climb, so a fall off
  a platform kills. One solution: each step is the only reachable next platform (the other platforms are out of reach
  from it, checked by the reach validator), and a mover must be ridden at its near end. Band 1 limits apply (no
  precision).
- **The door moves down onto the climb's top platform**, low enough that an upside-down cat on the roof can't reach it
  (door top below the roof's underside − 0.56 − 1.6).
- **Spikes on the roof's underside** over the door's end (the developer's "invert the spikes"), visible: a cat walking
  the roof upside down has to take the down-flip to the slab before it gets there.
- `DoorUprightTests` gets L004.

### 3.3 L007: fix the floating spikes, then make it hard

- **The floating spikes:** PAX-102 put each S2 gap's kill strip at S2's own height, with nothing under it (the gaps open
  onto S1, 4 u below). Fix: remove both strips; instead, hidden spikes on S1's top under each gap, set off once the cat
  has climbed past S1 (chained from the right tower's top), so they're up and visible through the gaps by the time the
  cat rides across S2. A fall off a rider lands on them.
- **The storm on S2:** one storm cloud wakes as the cat comes onto S2 and follows it along S2. The riders make the cat
  wait; the cloud punishes waiting. Each wait spot gets room to step out of a strike's column during the charge (the
  dodge rule, D-090 (7)), so it's hard but fair: watch the rider and the cloud together.
- **Study the path:** the ground and S1 stretches are re-checked for dead time (long waits where nothing threatens). Where
  a wait has no pressure, the next trap is moved into it or the rhythm is tightened. Proposals listed in Phase 2 before
  they're built.

### 3.4 L009: spikes on the roof's underside over the door

- Visible spikes along Roof_L's underside over Slab_D (x 0 to Flip_E's west side), so an upside-down cat can't get above
  the door. Every route that finishes still finishes standing (`DoorUprightTests`).

### 3.5 A new check: no door reachable upside down

- `ValidateDoorUpright` (band 1 and 2 levels with a gravity flip): for every ceiling underside a gravity-up cat can stand
  on, the door must be out of its reach (a jump of 1.6 down from the underside, with the cat's 0.56 height), unless that
  underside is a hazard. Red first on today's L004 and L009 (both reachable). Reports, for band 2, whatever it finds,
  without changing band 2 levels (they go to their own tickets).

### 3.6 The storm in band 1 (D-090 amendment)

- D-090 amended: the storm cloud may appear in levels 5–10, not every level. Proposed: **L007 and L010** (L007 by the
  developer's note; L010 as band 1's finale). L005, L006, L008 and L009 stay without one unless the developer adds them.
- Band 1 tuning: D-090's defaults (follow 0.08 u a tick, tell 25, strike every 100) and its dodge rule unchanged; at most
  one per room.
- `ValidateBand`'s "a cloud in a level numbered 1–10 is an error" becomes "1–4".

## 8. As built (2026-10-03)

The developer's answers: storms in L007 and L010 only; no retreat on L004's climb (keep it for a later level); one low
level arrow to jump over in L003, with a safe spot between launchers.

- **The new check (D-109):** `ValidateDoorUpright` (`LevelLayoutValidator.DoorUpright.cs`), run by `Band1LevelTests`.
  Red first on L004 and L009; `Band1RulesTests` checks it on a fixture room (a bare roof fails, a spiked one passes).
- **L009:** visible spikes along Roof_L's underside from the wall to Flip_E (x 0-7.5). A standing cat's jump on Slab_D tops
  out at 13.16, under their tips at 13.7.
- **L004:** the climb. Flip_E, the door pads, the door's retreat and the Roof_5 recess are gone. Six 1 × 0.5 platforms in a
  line up and to the left: Step_1, Ride_2, Step_3, Ride_4, Step_5, Door_Step (the door at x 0.5 by the wall), each 0.82 u
  above the last (two steps is 1.64, past a jump's 1.6: none can be skipped), none over another's jumps. Ride_2 and Ride_4
  move 1.5 u left and back (Carry, Periodic 150, phases 0 and 75). Visible spikes cover the slab's top under the climb (x
  0-11.25) and the roof's underside over it (x 0-12.75); a cat that jumps on the door step meets them. Each hop onto a 1 u
  platform is a timed mid-air release (windows 13-31); each ride is boarded as it comes home. New betrayals T9 and T10 (hop
  for a ride after it has left: the climb's spikes). Solution 905 ticks. `DoorUprightTests` and `DoorStandsTests` cover it.
  (A zig-zag was tried first: its upper platforms hung over the lower ones' jumps.)
- **L003:** S2 is the arrow floor; the ground's and S1's arrows and S2's falling block (T5) are gone. Four launchers:
  Arrow_2A (−60°, every 120, corbel at x 8.5), Arrow_2C (level, at the cat's height, every 180: from a low plinth at x
  16.75-17.75 to a low block at x 12.75-13.25; hop the block, cross or jump a shot in flight, hop the plinth), Arrow_2D
  (−45°, every 240, corbel at x 24) and Arrow_2B (−30°, every 120, corbel at x 29.75: low only at x 24.2-25.5, then over the
  cat, stopping short of the door). A safe spot before each lane; the route waits for each shot to land (from the arrow's
  home, past its tell). The level arrow passes D-056's slack (its lane is 3.5 u). Betrayals T8-T11, one per launcher.
- **L007:** the floating kill strips are gone. Hidden spikes on S1 under the gaps (and on the overhang's top under Sink_S)
  come up as the cat steps onto S2 (`Spikes_GapA`, with `Spikes_GapB` and `Spikes_GapO` chained; a chain can't start from

  Ride_A over gap A and follows from S2's east end; Ride_B is phased 110 ticks after Ride_A (phase 120), so a cat that walks
  off Ride_A boards it just before it leaves: from Ride_A on, the way is timed, and waiting is struck (T14).

  stair's stub, as the cat lands on Ledge_E1; Sink_E gives way; the storm wakes over the gap and strikes a cat that stops
  short of the door (T10).
- **The storm in band 1 (D-090 amendment):** `ValidateBand` allows clouds from level 5; `StormCloudValidatorTests` covers
  levels 1-4 (error) and 5/10 (pass).
- **Re-pinned:** `ShippedRouteResultsTests` for L003, L004, L007 and L010 (L009's results are unchanged).

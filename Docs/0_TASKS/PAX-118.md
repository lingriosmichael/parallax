# PAX-118 · Swinging pendulum blades (D-197 (2))

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-24. The developer picked these mechanics from a list of thirteen: "1 ... 6 ... 7 ... 9 ... 10".
**Phase 1 size:** Lite. Each builds on an existing path (the RoomTrap timing, the spinner, the carry, the push, the arrow lane), as
D-187's did.
**Decisions:** D-040, D-091, D-187, D-197.

## 1. What to build

1. **Pendulum spinner:** a `SpinnerSettings` swing amplitude and period. The arm swings `centre ± amplitude` as a sine of
   room ticks (or of ticks since its fire), and doesn't turn round. One arm from a pivot above, by default hanging down.
2. **Runtime:** `SpinnerTrap.AngleAt` gains the swing. Kills, the harness and the restore are unchanged.
3. **Validator:**
   - The peak tip speed (amplitude in radians × 2π / period × length) is ≤ 0.5 u a tick.
   - The swept arc's disc follows the spinner rules.
4. **Tests:** the swing math, a rule, and a fixture room (pass under it while it's at the far end of its swing).

`TrapPath.cs`, `SpinnerTrap.cs`, `SoloRoomElementTypes.cs`, `TrapKitSetup.Spinner.cs`, `LevelLayoutValidator.NewMovers.cs`, new tests, `Docs/07_DECISIONS.md`, this ticket.

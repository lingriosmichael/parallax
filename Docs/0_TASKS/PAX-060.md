## 1. The developer's brief (2026-09-26, in their words where possible)

> "I need you to be very creative. From level 11 every level needs a unique type of challenge and methodology of solving. I need creativity, and I need each level to be longer. And ultimately I expect utter chaos for each level. I want unique level design."

What that means for this ticket, so it can be checked:
1. **One verb per level, never repeated.** Each level is solved by a different way of thinking (the concepts' table: build, unlearn, count, go down, keep moving, two surfaces, plan the way back, get above, execute a memorised map, ride the machine). If a sketch's learned solution could be described with another level's verb, it's wrong.
2. **Longer:** 30–50 s once known (1500–2500 ticks), in 2–3 checkpoint sections (D-091), each section ≤ 1000 ticks (aim ≤ 750).
3. **Utter chaos, choreographed:** every level has at least one **chaos moment**: a fixed chain in which at least **5 elements visibly change within 60 ticks** on the solution route. It's the same on every attempt (D-040, D-067): panic on the first try, a dance by the tenth.
4. **Unique design:** no two levels share a layout skeleton (for example "corridor with a pit every 4 u"), and no level is a corridor of traps in a line. Every level has height, branches and dead ends worth exploring.
5. **Hard but readable:** extremely hard is allowed (band 2), unfair isn't. Every death shows its cause (P11). No tells (P10).

**Creativity is part of the acceptance.** The Architect will send back a sketch that's valid but flat: correct counts, but a filler trap, a repeated answer, or a chaos moment that is just "many traps at once" with no idea behind it. Improving a concept is welcome; replacing its verb or its chaos moment needs a ruling.

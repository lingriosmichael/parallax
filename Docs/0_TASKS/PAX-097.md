# PAX-097 · L008 Lift_3: make the spike contact a clear kill

**Status:** Draft 2026-09-30 (from the PAX-A08 room audit); needs the Architect's approval. Not part of PAX-A08.
**Phase 1 size: Lite.**
**Depends on:** nothing open. **Decisions:** D-052 (the collider stays 1.0 × 0.56), D-058 (the hold), D-079/D-080 (route
pins), D-085 (band 1: lethal traps).

## 1. Why

L008's betrayal T3 ("the lift carries a cat that walks onto it into the roof's spikes") kills by a hairline. Lift_3 rises
3.2 u to a top of 13.2, so a cat standing on it has its collider top at 13.76. Spikes_3's underside is at 13.70, so they
overlap by only **0.06 u** (`L008Layout.cs:42–43`; the PAX-A08 room audit, 2026-09-30). Any skin width, contact offset or
tick rounding could turn the kill into a miss, and a future motor or collider change would break T3 without a clear
signal.

## 2. What to decide (Phase 1)

T3 is meant to kill, so the likely answer is a clear kill, not a clear miss:
- **Clear kill (recommended):** raise the lift's end pose or lower Spikes_3 so the carried cat overlaps the spikes by a
  margin (for example at least 0.15 u), keeping the ceiling above the lift's end pose clear of the solid (today 0.80 u).
- **Clear miss:** only if T3 is re-designed; the betrayal would need a new killer.

Either way the change moves route pins (T3's timing and lead), so it re-pins L008 through the route tests.

## 3. Tests

- A layout test: the carried cat's overlap with Spikes_3 at the lift's end pose is at least the chosen margin.
- L008's route results re-pinned; the band-1 rules and the camera-tell rule still pass.

## 4. Allowed files

`Assets/_Game/Editor/Levels/L008Layout.cs`, `L008Routes.cs` (if the pins move), the route-result pins, tests.
The developer rebuilds L008 (Rebuild All Levels) and saves.

## 5. Out of scope

Other levels; the collider, motor and tick timing; a general margin rule (that is PAX-098's clearance rule, or a later
lift-into-hazard rule).

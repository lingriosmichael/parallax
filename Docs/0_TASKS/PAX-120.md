# PAX-120 · Portals (D-197 (4))

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-24. The developer picked these mechanics from a list of thirteen: "1 ... 6 ... 7 ... 9 ... 10".
**Phase 1 size:** Lite. Each builds on an existing path (the RoomTrap timing, the spinner, the carry, the push, the arrow lane), as
D-187's did.
**Decisions:** D-040, D-091, D-187, D-197.

## 1. What to build

1. **`SoloRoomElementKind.Portal`** (appended): an entry box (the element) and an exit box (its secondary box).
   - A cat whose centre enters the entry is moved by exit − entry, keeping its velocity (`CatMotor2D.ApplyPush`).
   - It can't go back through until it has left the exit (no ping-pong).
   - One-way by default; `twoWay` makes the exit an entry too.
2. **Moving exit:** the exit can follow a `PathSettings` (smooth or glitch, Periodic). This is the troll: time your entry, or come
   out over spikes. Its pose is a function of ticks (D-091).
3. **Look:** two coloured ovals. The exit flickers through a glitch warning.
4. **Validator:**
   - Both boxes sit inside the room, clear of fixed solids, and are tall and wide enough for the cat.
   - The exit (at every pose) doesn't overlap a hazard.
   - Static reach models treat the portal as a link, entry to exit.
5. **Tests:** the teleport and kept velocity, no ping-pong, the moving exit, and a fixture route through it.

# PAX-113 · Sweeping scanner (D-172 (3))

**Status:** Draft 2026-10-23, waiting for the developer's approval. Not built.
**Phase 1 size:** Full (a moving kill volume on the moving-trap timing; clean-falls, coverage and camera rules must accept it).
**Decisions:** D-055, D-091, D-140 (slam kills), D-172.

## 1. Why

Obsidian Void (L031+) needs a moving threat that fits its world. A scanner is a vertical laser beam, from a floor emitter to a
ceiling emitter, that slides along a track across a storey and back. It's a chase or a timing beat, like a slam moving sideways.

## 2. What to build

1. **A lethal beam body for `MovingTrap`.** A `MovingTrapKind` value is appended (e.g. `Beam`): the body isn't solid and never
   carries the cat, and its box is lethal on every tick while it isn't home. It reuses the moving-trap timing as it is (offset,
   move, hold, return, repeat modes, triggers, chains).
2. **Warning:** for ≥ 6 ticks before it leaves home, the beam flickers at home and is harmless.
3. **Runtime:** the moving-trap runtime draws the beam and tests its box against the cat each tick. Snapshot and restore stay as
   they are for moving traps (D-091).
4. **Validator:**
   - the whole sweep (start to end box) stays inside the frame;
   - the beam spans floor to ceiling at every point of its path (no gap to duck under unless the design declares one);
   - clean falls and trigger coverage treat it as a hazard, not a surface;
   - any route that dies to it must have it on camera before the hold ends (D-083).
5. **Trap Lab:** a scanner chasing along a corridor to a door, and a scanner to wait out under an overhang.

## 3. Allowed files (Phase 1 confirms)

- `SoloRoomElementTypes.cs` (`MovingTrapKind`)
- The moving-trap runtime and builder files (named in Phase 1)
- The moving-floor, clean-falls and coverage validators (named in Phase 1)
- Trap Lab layout and routes
- EditMode tests: a new `ScannerTests`, plus the touched validator tests
- `Docs/07_DECISIONS.md`, this ticket

## 4. Out of scope

- Angled or rotating beams.
- Horizontal scanners moving up and down (a later ticket if wanted).
- Final art (PAX-A20).
- Any L001–L030 change.

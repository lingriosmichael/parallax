## 1. Why

The developer's play-test and rulings (2026-10-03):
- "Level 3 is way too easy, add 4 more arrow launchers and make them shoot at intervals." "Level 4 is too easy."
- "Level 8 is too easy. No moving platforms, same for Level 7. I want gaps where you have to jump onto a moving platform
  to get from left to right, and add arrow launchers on both."
- L009 and L010: "add an inverter … and a platform on the very left to reach the door. I don't want to be able to solve
  this level by jumping on a door while inverted." "Level 10 is too easy. More moving platforms."
- "Falling blocks should leave a hole." The L010 haze box.

## 2. Rules for every level here

- **Arrows** use only the supported angles (0, ±30°, ±45°, ±60°; no vertical arrow). A repeating arrow crosses the walk on a
  short, steep lane (±60° or ±45°), never along it (D-056).
- **One rhythm per level.** All repeating arrows in a level share one period, or simple ratios of it (120 and 240), with
  phase offsets, so the pattern repeats and can be learned.
- **Band 1 stays band 1:** no precision sections, the band 1 reach and slack, 500–1500 ticks for the solution, dead ends
  that kill, every new element with its betrayal (Dies, the killer, lead ≥ 6, or Recovers).
- **No painted hole on a walkable floor**, ever.
- Exact positions, periods and phases are set in Phase 2 against the validators. Anything that won't fit is reported,
  not forced.

### The two stops, ruled (2026-10-03)

1. **Real holes vs D-085: D-085 amended.** A falling block may be a section of a split slab: floors on both sides
   touching it with the same top and bottom, drawn as one surface (no seam, outline or colour change around the block).
   `ValidateBand1Tells` fails on any exposed side edge of the skin at the block's edges (a seam), and on a block that is
   neither flush in a solid nor such a section. `Band1RulesTests`: a split slab passes; a 0.2 u step east of the block is
   rejected (seen red). Applied:
   - **L002 (reversed by the developer's play-test, 2026-10-03: "If part of the floor falls, there should be a hole
     there"; S1 rework approved):** Block_1–3 are 1.6 u sections of S1 (x 20.2–21.8, 15.8–17.4, 10.4–12). Block_1 moved
     0.5 u east (its trigger and the bait stop with it), Block_2 0.2 u west (a wider trigger, delay 9), Block_3 0.2 u
     east (still over the Ledge). Spikes_4 moved west and narrowed to 1 u (x 7.25–8.25), set off from Tread_C's top so
     a strip remains before Block_3's hole (its lead is 50 ticks now, was 11: the spikes are up before the cat reaches
     S1). The door's end of S1 moved 1.5 u east (door 23.5, retreat to 27; Floor_9 x 23.8–25.4). Spikes_6 trimmed to
     S1_A3 (x 18.5–20.2), clear of Block_1's hole. The retreat trigger moved onto S1_A2 (x 15.5). The ground run now
     hops onto the fallen Block_1 and off it short of Block_2; on S1 the cat hops each hole (timed releases, windows
     15–32). Solution 572 ticks; every band 1 rule passes.
   - **L010 Block_1:** a real hole, 1.6 u wide (Shelf x 12.5–14.2, the block 14.2–15.8, Shelf_E 15.8–16.8, widened to
     band 1's 1 u minimum). Its routes pass unchanged; solution 685 ticks.
   - **L008 Block_6:** a real hole, 1.6 u wide (S1_W x 4–11.2, the block 11.2–12.8, S1 12.8–28; Spikes_5 moved east of
     it, x 13.25–14.75). Its trigger stays on the ground storey (x 9.2–13.6, y 0–4), so the S1 walk never sets it off;
     the coverage check now counts a slab-section block as the slab over its storey until it falls
     (`TriggerCoverage.TryStoreyCeiling`). First attempt (trigger up to S1) failed T6; this one passes.
2. **Roof-block art holes: `SolidArt` and `TrapArtSetup` added to this ticket.** A roof block gets a `Socket` child
   (`ENV_Socket`, sized to the block, one order above it, off at rest), drawn by `SolidArt` only once the block is its
   own height away from its place, like the crack. A roof block = a block in the room's highest ceiling, in a room with
   no gravity flip. `TrapArtParityTests` checks the hole never shows before the block has left and always shows after.
   The rebuild created sockets on L001 Block_1–2, L003 Block_5, L006 Block_2, L008 Block_1, L015 Block_1–3, **and L017
   Block_6** (a roof block by the same rule; not in the approved list, so it needs the developer's OK or an exclusion).

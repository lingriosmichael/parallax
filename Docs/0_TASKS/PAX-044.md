## 1. Goal

PAX-043 proved the traps work in real rooms, but its playtest verdict was **far too easy**. Two
betrayals per room is a demo. This ticket rebuilds the same four rooms at Level Devil density:
**4–5 chained betrayals per room**, where every solution lands the player in the next trap.

- **Same kit, no new gameplay code.** Everything below is built from the PAX-042 traps, the
  PAX-041 hazard and plain geometry.
- **Kit gaps get recorded, not built.** Anything the rooms want that the kit can't do goes on the
  gap list (§8). That list becomes PAX-045 (trap kit v2).

## 2. Movement facts (measured at the PAX-043 gate)

Run speed 6 u/s, gravity 30 u/s², jump height 3.2 u (take-off ≈ 13.9 u/s), full-speed jump reach
**R ≈ 5.54 u**, air time ≈ 0.92 s. Cat collider 1.0 × 0.56, paw line −0.4. Door 0.6 × 1.5.
Collapse delay 12 ticks (0.24 s) = 1.44 u of running. Falling blocks here all use **0.3 u/tick**
(15 u/s).

**Maximum flip drift:** a cat flipped by a floor-level zone rises 7 u in ≈ 0.68 s and can move
≈ 4.1 u sideways on the way. Used by rule 4 below.

**Jump travel (contract for §4 and test 8):** all x values are the **cat's centre**. Take-off is the
last centre position where the cat is fully supported (near edge − half-width); landing is the
first fully supported, or fully clear of the hazard, past it (far edge + half-width). So every
jump, pit or hazard, needs **gap + collider width** (1.0 u) of travel: a 3 u pit or a 2 u spike
strip is a 4 u or 3 u jump. Heights are **paw heights** in the jump's gravity frame (for ceiling
walkers, measured down from the ceiling underside).

## 3. Layout rules (lessons from PAX-043, now mandatory)

1. **Collapsing sections are full slabs**, the same thickness and top/underside as the surface
   they replace (floor 1 u, ceiling 1 u, ledge 0.5 u). No bumps, no notches.
2. **Pit and recess walls sit outside the opening**, never overlapping the opening's x-range. No
   ledge inside a pit or recess.
3. **Every pit and recess is closed**: side walls, plus a bottom (pit) or cap (recess), with a hazard
   strip across the full width.
4. **Every hidden `ForceUp` zone has ceiling hazard** covering its x-range ± (4.1 u drift + 0.5 u cat
   half-width). A cat thrown up by a betrayal always dies. It can never end up alive on the
   ceiling with no way down.
5. **Every flip tool re-arms** (`rearmOnExit` true), so any flip can be undone.
6. **Trap triggers stay clear of the spawn footprint** (as PAX-043).
7. **All trap settings live in the layout data**: delay, travel, speed, direction, mode, re-arm,
   offset, renderer on/off. No name-based or room-id-based rules in `SoloRoomsSetup`. **Tuning
   happens in the layout data and the scene is always rebuilt from it** (D-054). Inspector tweaks
   in Play mode are for finding a value only; they are never saved to the scene.
8. **Hidden spikes the player must jump** are at most 2 u wide, and their near edge is at least
   2.5 u past the far edge of their trigger. Arriving at full speed still dies; stopping leaves a
   run-up.

## 4. Rooms

Same frame as PAX-043 (floor top 0, ceiling underside 7, walls, checkpoint at x 2, gravity down),
but **W = 32** and origins at **x = 0, 45, 90, 135** (gap 13 u). Room ids stay 0–3; the last door
still ends the level (`LevelComplete`). Disguise rule unchanged: betrayals look like their
surroundings, tools are purple, visible hazards are red. All x values are room-local.

All values are starting points. Timings marked **tune** are adjusted in acceptance until the
stated kill/survive condition holds.

Each room lists its **required jumps**. They go into the layout data as `RequiredJumps` records
and feed test 8. Fields: take-off centre x and paw height, landing centre x and paw height,
runway length (from rest), gravity frame (`Floor` or `Ceiling`), kind (`Pit` or `Hazard`), and for
hazards the hazard's near/far edge and height. The records also reference the element edges they
come from, so the test can recompute the x values (§6).

## 6. Tests (EditMode)

Update `SoloRoomsLayoutTests` for v2 and turn the §3 rules into data checks:

1. Spawn safety (footprint from `CatMotorConfig`, not typed constants).
2. Containment: every element inside its room, with bounds derived from each room's own pits and
   recesses (no room-id special cases).
3. Separation: origins ≥ W + 10 apart; one checkpoint and one door per room.
4. Rule 1: every collapsing section matches the thickness and top/underside of its surface.
5. Rule 2: no wall overlaps the x-range of the opening it bounds.
6. Rule 4: every hidden `ForceUp` zone has ceiling hazard over its x-range ± 4.6 u, clipped to the
   room. The drift (4.1) is one named constant, not repeated.
7. Rule 5: every visible flip tool re-arms.
8. **Reachability**, per `RequiredJumps` record, using the §2 contract. `runSpeed`, ground
   acceleration, `jumpHeight` and collider width come from `CatMotorConfig`; `g` comes from
   `GravityReceiver.Strength` on `Cat_Player.prefab` (load the prefab, no typed constant).
   - **Positions:** recompute take-off x (near edge − half-width) and landing x (far edge +
     half-width, direction-signed) from the referenced elements; fail if they differ from the
     record. D = |landing x − take-off x|.
   - **Take-off speed:** `v_y = sqrt(2 · g · jumpHeight)`. `v_x = runSpeed` if the motor has no
     finite ground acceleration, else `min(runSpeed, sqrt(2 · accel · runway))`, from rest. If the
     motor's ground model is not constant acceleration, **stop and report** before writing the
     test.
   - **Flight:** `Δh` = landing paw height − take-off paw height, in the record's gravity frame.
     Unreachable if `v_y² < 2 · g · Δh`. Otherwise `T = (v_y + sqrt(v_y² − 2 · g · Δh)) / g`,
     `reach = v_x · T`. No air control assumed.
   - **Pass:** `D ≤ 0.75 · reach`.
   - **Hazard clearance** (kind `Hazard`): with hazard height `h` and centre span
     `w = far − near`, `window = v_x · 2 · sqrt(v_y² − 2 · g · h) / g`; pass if
     `w ≤ 0.75 · window`.
   - **Steps** (`RequiredSteps`): rise ≤ 0.8 × `jumpHeight`.
   - **Unjumpable floor hazards:** length + collider width > R (full speed, Δh 0).
   - Print a table per record: D, v_x, T, reach, ratio, and window for hazards. If a record
     fails, **stop and report** the numbers; don't move geometry to make it pass. This is a sanity
     gate on the data, not a physics simulation: §10.2 in the Editor decides.
9. Rule 8: every jumped `HiddenSpikesTrap` is ≤ 2 u wide and its near edge is ≥ 2.5 u past its
   trigger's far edge.

**Baseline:** the PAX-043 total. Report baseline → new.

## 10. Acceptance (Editor)

1. **Each betrayal fires as written** and kills the obvious response at full speed.
2. **Each solution works** 3 times in a row. Report final values for every **tune** item.
3. **No soft-locks:** in every room, try to get stuck, including flipping at the edges of each
   hidden zone and each tool, and **jumping from the ceiling next to tool A in Rooms 2 and 4**
   (the jump reaches down into the tool). Any state you can't leave (other than by dying) is a
   failure.
4. **No ledges:** falling into any pit or up into any recess always kills.
5. **Disguise:** no collapse, block or hidden zone can be told apart before it fires.
6. **Difficulty:** the designer knows the solutions, so the real test is **someone who hasn't seen
   the rooms**, playing blind. Target: roughly 10–20 deaths per room. If no one is available,
   report your own deaths per room and mark the number as "designer run".
7. **Last door:** `LevelComplete` logs as before.
8. **Determinism:** the same inputs give the same result twice.
9. **Regression:** the `Sandbox_Realities` menus still report no change.
10. **Console:** known noise only.

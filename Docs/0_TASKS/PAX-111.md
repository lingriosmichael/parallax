# PAX-111 · Laser beam turret and hard-light bar (D-172 (3))

**Status:** Draft 2026-10-23, waiting for the developer's approval. Not built.
**Phase 1 size:** Full (a new runtime timing for an existing trap, a new kill shape, and new validator rules).
**Decisions:** D-078 (arrows), D-086 (spears), D-091 (sections), D-172 (Obsidian traps).

## 1. Why

The developer, 2026-10-23: "There are also no arrow launchers, they should also be lasers. The Lasers should show a tiny ray
before it fires, like a warning that it is coming." Obsidian Void levels (L031+) replace arrows with laser turrets and the lance
with a hard-light bar. Arrows and spears stay exactly as they are in L001–L030.

## 2. What to build

1. **Beam mode on the arrow lane.** `ArrowLane` gets a `Beam` flag and `BeamTicks`, appended to the struct, with defaults that
   reproduce today's arrow exactly. A beam shot:
   - **Tell** (`TellTicks`, ≥ 6, D-078): a thin warning ray covers the whole lane from the mouth to `LaneEndX`. It's harmless.
   - **Beam:** from the tell's end, the whole lane box (the lane's length × `Thickness`) is lethal for `BeamTicks` ticks. Then
     it's off.
   - **Timing:** repeats and cooldowns use `TrapTiming` exactly as arrows do (Once, Rearm, Continuous, Periodic).
   - **Kill test:** every tick, the lane box is tested against the cat, through the same `RoomDeath` path as an arrow hit.
   - **Pure math:** `ArrowMath` (Core) gains `BeamPhase(ticksSinceFire, tell, beam)` (Idle, Tell, Beam), unit-tested.
2. **Runtime.** `ArrowTrap` with `beam` set draws the ray during the tell and the beam while it's lethal (two child sprites,
   stretched along the lane), and places no moving arrow. Snapshot and restore work by formula from the fire tick (D-091).
3. **Hard-light bar** (the lance in Obsidian). It is D-086's spear: it fires once, sticks at the lane end, and its shaft becomes
   solid. It gets beam mode's warning ray during its tell. It's art only, so no new rule.
4. **Validator** (`LevelLayoutValidator.Arrows.cs`):
   - A beam's lane box is its kill volume while lethal (it feeds `KillVolumes`, camera tells and geyser envelopes).
   - `BeamTicks` ≥ 1.
   - Cooldown ≥ tell + beam.
   - A beam is never disguised (the ray is its tell).
   - Every existing lane check still applies.
5. **Route validator:** a beam's first visible change is the ray's first tick, so leads count from it, as for an arrow's tell.
6. **Trap Lab:** one room with a periodic beam across a walk (wait for it to go off, then cross), plus fixtures.

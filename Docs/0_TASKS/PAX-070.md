# PAX-070 · Closed test = blind playtest (Gate 3)

**Status:** Draft. Phase H.
**Depends on:** PAX-069 build on the closed testing track
**Decisions:** D-064 (4), Vision §13 (now the pass criteria)

## 1. Why

One outside test that serves both purposes: Google's closed-testing requirement for new
personal accounts, and the blind playtest the game has never had.

## 2. Setup

- Closed testing track, 15–25 opted-in testers (≥ 12 must stay opted in for 14 continuous days;
  a dip restarts the clock). Testers must actually play; Google checks engagement.
- Testers get the paid unlock free (licence-tester or a promo mechanism) so they can reach
  hard levels.
- No analytics in the app. Data comes from: a screenshot of the level-select screen (best
  deaths per level) at day 3 and day 14, and a short form (below).
- At least 3–5 testers are watched playing their first 10 minutes without help (in person or
  screen share): these are the true blind observations.

## 3. Form (keep it to 6 questions)

1. Furthest level reached. 2. After a death, could you say what killed you? (always / mostly /
rarely) 3. Did any death feel like the controls' fault? Which level? 4. Did you want to try
another level? 5. Where did you stop, and why? 6. Anything else.

## 4. Pass criteria (Vision §13)

Players retry immediately after deaths; they can say what the room did; they blame the room, not
the controls; they ask for more. Any level where ≥ 30% of testers blame the controls gets a fix
ticket.

## 5. Output

`Docs/Playtests/closed_test.md`: results, the Gate 3 verdict (record as D-035, reserved for it),
the list of fix tickets. Then apply for production access (Play Console questionnaire: how
testers were recruited, what feedback, what changed).

# PAX-112 · Pulse gate (D-172 (3))

**Status:** Draft 2026-10-23, waiting for the developer's approval. Not built.
**Phase 1 size:** Lite (a new element kind on the existing trap timing; no motor change).
**Decisions:** D-040, D-055 (Periodic), D-057 (tells), D-091 (sections), D-172.

## 1. Why

Obsidian Void (L031+) needs timing traps that fit its world. A pulse gate is a fixed laser barrier (a vertical or horizontal
bar between two emitters) that turns on and off on a beat. It's a door the player times.

## 2. What to build

1. **Element kind `PulseGate`**, appended to `SoloRoomElementKind`. It is a box (the barrier) between two emitter ends.
   Settings:
   - Periodic `periodTicks` and `phaseTicks`;
   - `warnTicks` (≥ 6): the barrier flickers, harmless;
   - `onTicks`: the barrier is lethal.
   - It's always Periodic (like geysers, D-088).
2. **Core:** `PulseGateMath.PhaseAt(roomTick, period, phase, warn, on)` → Off, Warn, On. Pure and unit-tested.
3. **Runtime:** `PulseGateTrap : RoomTrap`.
   - During On, the barrier box is tested against the cat each tick, and a hit kills through `RoomDeath`.
   - The look changes by phase (off: dim emitters; warn: flicker; on: beam).
   - Snapshot and restore work by formula from the room tick (D-091).
4. **Validator** (`LevelLayoutValidator.PulseGate.cs`, new):
   - warn ≥ 6;
   - warn + on < period;
   - the off gap is long enough for a run across the barrier's thickness plus the cat's width (computed from `CatMotorConfig`);
   - the barrier sits inside the frame and touches a solid at both ends;
   - it's a kill volume only while On (it feeds `KillVolumes`, camera tells and geyser envelopes).
5. **Builder and kit:** the builder places it, and `ValidateKit` runs its validator. It gets placeholder art.
6. **Trap Lab:** one room with a gate across a walk and one across a shaft.

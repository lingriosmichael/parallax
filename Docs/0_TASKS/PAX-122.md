# PAX-122 · Phase platform (D-201 (3))

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-25. The developer, on L021's glitch stone: "I prefer it to be just in one place, but appearing and
disppearing somewhere in the middle. I dont need it to move places, just one spot", then "Do 2 and 3 for now".
**Phase 1 size:** Lite. It's an option on the existing MovingTrap Solid, so the stone art (SolidArt mirrors the solid's look) and
the crush, kill attribution and rewind paths are reused.
**Decisions:** D-040, D-057, D-091, D-187, D-201.

## 1. What to build

1. **Settings:** `MovingFloorSettings.PhaseOnTicks` (0: none) and `PhaseWarnTicks`, on a MovingTrap Solid with Periodic timing.
2. **Runtime (`MovingTrap`):** the platform never moves. From each fire it is there for `phaseOnTicks`, then gone (no collider and
   not drawn) until the next fire.
   - **The tell:** it flickers for the last `phaseWarnTicks` of its time there.
   - **Appearing on the cat:** it kills a cat it appears on, by the crush depth, the same way as a glitch wall's arrival
     (`OwnKill`).
   - **Exact:** its state is a function of ticks since the fire (`TrapPath.PhaseState`), so a reset and a rewind restore it
     exactly.
3. **Validator (`CheckPhase`):**
   - It is a Solid on Periodic timing.
   - It has no path, move, hinge or flip.
   - Its flicker lasts at least 6 ticks.
   - It is there for at least its flicker plus 24 ticks, and gone for at least 12 ticks each period.
   - Its cooldown covers its time there.
   - It never appears inside a fixed solid.
4. **Tests (`Kit2Tests`):**
   - The state math.
   - The fixture room passes every rule.
   - A short flicker is rejected.
   - The fixture's routes pass through the real game code: the solution, a betrayal (stay on it as it goes), and an exact
     rewind.

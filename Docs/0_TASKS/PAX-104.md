## 7. Pit10_Cover (L010): diagnosed, not fixed

**The failure:** at T7's reveal tick (221), Pit10_Cover's frame differs from the tick before in 90 of 98304 pixels
(worst 77/255). The test expects a fake floor to look the same until it reveals.

**The cause, measured:** it isn't Pit10_Cover that changes. Pit10_Cover (a FakePlatform, x 3–6, the world-tiled
`ENV_Fill_A3` skin) renders one semi-transparent 1-pixel column at world x = 4.0, exactly on a render-pixel boundary
(pixel 128 at 128 px/u). Drop_10 (a MovingTrap behind it, sorting order −2, sinking 0.1 u per tick) shows through that
column, so two consecutive frames differ.
- Hiding Drop_10 makes the test pass. Shifting the camera half a pixel removes the column.
- Ruled out: the texture's alpha (opaque; imported ASTC 2308², unpacked, Repeat, no mips), atlas packing, MSAA (off
  doesn't change it), the shard cuts (the nearest is at x 3.969), and a tile-wrap seam (the tile is 11.74 u, no wrap
  falls there). A wrap-seam nudge in `TrapArtSetup` was tried and reverted.

So it's a rasterisation artifact at an exact pixel alignment, not a disguise leak a player can see in motion: one
column, one pixel wide, at one camera pose.

**Options (the developer's ruling):**
- (a) **The test hides the other traps' bodies** while it compares a trap's frames (it checks the disguise of one trap,
  not what moves behind it). This changes the R8 rule; needs the developer's OK.
- (b) **A follow-up art/rendering ticket** on the world-tiled skin's mesh (abutting quads, or the tiling shader's UVs at
  a pixel boundary). The real fix, but an unknown size.
- (c) **A layout tweak in L010** so nothing moves behind Pit10_Cover (Drop_10 moved or reordered). Cheap, but it hides
  the artifact rather than fixing it, and L010 is out of this ticket's scope.

Recommended: (a) now (the test then checks what it's meant to check) with (b) logged for the art backlog.

**Ruled (the developer, 2026-10-04):**
- **(a), built:** `TrapArtRevealFrameTests` hides every other trap's bodies while it compares one element's frames (each
  other trap's whole art, skin, trims and launcher, and its grey-box sprites), as it already hid the cat and every
  effect. Hiding only the `Bodies` sprites wasn't enough: Drop_10's skin trims (Cap, Under) still showed (74 pixels). The rule is recorded in the test's header
  comment. It checks the element's own disguise; what moves behind it is no longer its business.
- **(b), logged as `Docs/V_TASKS/PAX-V09.md`:** the column is a potential in-game tell (D-085: a moving trap shows through
  a disguise), not only a test artifact. Scope: find every tile boundary and camera pose where it shows, then fix the skin
  drawing, with a permanent scan test. Scheduled before PAX-A17 or as its first item (step 0 in the A17 draft).

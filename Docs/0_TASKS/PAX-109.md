# PAX-109 · Trap art tests follow D-153 amendment 5 (fittings show from the fire)

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-21 (the developer: "A small ticket to fix the 27 old slam-plate and hinge-knuckle test failures,
then one full test run. And then we commit all"). Built the same day (§6).
**Phase 1 size:** None (test and presentation bookkeeping; no runtime behaviour changes).
**Decisions:** D-153 amendment 5 ("The slam's plate and the hinge floor's knuckle show from the trap's fire"), PAX-A13 §12 R8.

## 1. Why

Since c2ebe0b (D-153 amendment 5), `SolidArt.Fittings` hides a slam's plate and a hinge floor's knuckle until the trap
fires, so at rest the trap is the stone it's disguised as. Two art fixtures were written before that rule and still
expect the old behaviour, and they fail on every level with a slam or hinge floor (27 failures):
- **`TrapArtParityTests`** (13 levels plus `Parity_CatchesABlocksLandingDustOnItsReleaseTick`): every body must draw
  exactly when its grey-box shows. A resting plate or knuckle isn't drawn while its grey-box shows.
- **`TrapArtRevealFrameTests`** (13 levels): the frame on the reveal tick must equal the frame before it. The plate or
  knuckle appears on the fire tick.

The game is right (an accepted decision); the tests are stale.

## 2. What to build

1. `TrapArt.Body` gets `FromFire` (default false): a fitting drawn with its grey-box only from the trap's fire on.
   `SolidArt` marks its plate and knuckle bodies `fromFire: true`.
2. **Parity:** a `FromFire` body must **not** be drawn before its trap fires, which is now checked and fails if it is. From the
   fire on, the usual rule applies (drawn exactly with its grey-box, inside its bounds).
3. **Reveal frame:** the element's `FromFire` bodies are left out of both frames, like the effects that start on the
   reveal tick. Parity checks when they show.

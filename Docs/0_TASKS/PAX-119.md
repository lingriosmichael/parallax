# PAX-119 · Pressure plates and gates (D-197 (3))

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-24. The developer picked these mechanics from a list of thirteen: "1 ... 6 ... 7 ... 9 ... 10".
**Phase 1 size:** Lite. Each builds on an existing path (the RoomTrap timing, the spinner, the carry, the push, the arrow lane), as
D-187's did.
**Decisions:** D-040, D-091, D-187, D-197.

## 1. What to build

1. **`SoloRoomElementKind.Plate`** (appended): a thin plate flush on a floor top. A cat standing on it fires it (Overlap,
   Rearm), and it dips while pressed.
   - It's a chain source: gates, trapdoors and any trap can chain from it.
   - Gates are ordinary Solid movers chained from a plate, opening by their offset for their hold and closing over their return.
     A closing gate slams (D-140).
   - A second plate can chain a gate's close: a gate whose Chain source is the second plate.
2. **Runtime:** `PlateTrap : RoomTrap`; the press look is a function of its timing.
3. **Validator:** a plate is flush on a fixed top and as wide as at least the cat. Its chained targets keep every existing
   chain rule.
4. **Tests:** the press and fire, a fixture room (step on the plate and run through the gate before it shuts; a cat under it is
   crushed), and a rule.

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-02 (the developer: "Proceed with PAX 101"). PAX-099 and PAX-100 are built and await the developer's play-test and commit.
**Wait after Phase 1:** post the per-level plan and stop.
**Phase 1 size: Lite** (one plan table, no traces).
**Depends on:** PAX-099, PAX-100. **Decisions:** D-106, D-079/D-080, D-083, D-093 (band 2, chaos), D-069/D-083
(precision sections), D-091 (sections), D-095, D-104.

## 1. Why

As PAX-100, for the hard band. Here the new elements may also tighten execution inside precision sections (D-069), and
repeating arrows and riders can share a section's rhythm.

## 2. What to build

1. **Phase 1 plan:** one row per level (L011–L020), as in PAX-100: additions, intervals and angles, placement, and how it
   fits the level's theme (D-093). Levels with movers today (L015, L017–L020) are listed with what changes, if anything.
   Post it and stop.
2. **Build** each approved row: layout data, solution, betrayal routes, sections and precision thresholds as needed.
3. Rebuild the levels with `Rebuild All Levels`.

## 3. Tests

Each changed level passes `Band2LevelTests`, `Band2RouteResultsTests`, `ValidateRoutesTests`, the camera tell, the chaos
rule and `TrapArtRevealFrameTests`. The full batch suite runs once at the end.

## 4. Allowed files

`Assets/_Game/Editor/Levels/L011Layout.cs` … `L020Layout.cs`, `L011Routes.cs` … `L020Routes.cs`, the Band 2 route
results fixture, `LevelCameras.cs` (only if a change needs a different zoom), their tests, `Docs/07_DECISIONS.md`
(only if a ruling is needed).

## 5. Out of scope

L001–L010; kit changes; the A16 art.

## 6. Phase 1 plan (2026-10-02, approved: "Approve for now, will make changes later once I play them"; build, compile, hand over)

Levels 11–20 already carry their theme element, D-093's chaos and answer-tag rules, and pinned route results, and six of
them have moving floors already (L014 Shrink; L015 Mover_M; L017 Slide_4; L018 Drop_3; L019 Mover_1, Mover_2; L020 Gate_1,
Push_3, Shrink_8). No level here has a repeating or an angled arrow yet. So the plan adds those where they fit the
level's verb, and adds a floor only where none exists. Each addition gets an answer-tagged betrayal (D-093). PAX-100's
lesson applies: a repeating arrow gets a short crossing lane (an angled drip), never a long lane along the walk. Exact
positions are set in Phase 2; a row that won't fit is reported, not forced. Band2RouteResultsTests are re-pinned in the
final suite.

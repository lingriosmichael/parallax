## 1. Why

Geysers let a room go vertical: an air vent that erupts on a rhythm and throws the cat far higher than its 1.6 u jump, to reach high platforms. The rhythm is the puzzle; what's up there can be the troll (a launch into hidden ceiling spikes is allowed, and must be revealed like any betrayal).

## 2. What to build

1. **Element `Geyser`** (appended). A vent hosted flush in a Floor (direction Up) or a Ceiling (direction Down, for gravity-up rooms). Column: `ColumnWidth` (default 1.0), `ColumnHeight` (default 1.5) measured from the vent face. Always **Periodic** (D-055 (2)): `PeriodTicks`, `PhaseTicks`, `TellTicks` (default 25, ≥ 6), `EruptTicks` (default 40).
2. **Cycle, pure function of room ticks** (`GeyserMath` in Core): idle → **tell** (vent bubbles: a visible sprite change, harmless, no push) → **erupt** (column active) → idle. Periodic from room start plus phase, like every periodic trap.
3. **The push.** While erupting, if the cat's collider overlaps the column, its velocity component along the geyser's direction is **set** (absolute, not added) to `LaunchSpeed` (default **14 u/s** = 0.28 u/tick). Horizontal velocity is untouched, so the player can steer. When the cat leaves the column top it flies ballistically: rise above the column = v²/2g = 14² / 60 ≈ **3.27 u**, so the apex is ~4.8 u above the vent (the normal jump is 1.6).
   - New motor seam: `CatMotor2D.ApplyLaunch(Vector2 direction, float speed)`, applied inside the room's trap phase so it's deterministic in Play and in the harness. A launch clears grounded and consumes coyote (no coyote jump mid-air after a launch); a buffered jump still lands normally afterwards.
   - The push is not lethal. The geyser never touches `Physics2D.gravity`.
4. **Validator** (separately named):
   - `ValidateGeyser`: tell ≥ 6; erupt ≥ 1; `TellTicks + EruptTicks < PeriodTicks`; the vent sits in a Floor/Ceiling face; the column overlaps no solid element.
   - `ValidateGeyserEnvelope`: the launch envelope (column plus rise plus cat height, column width plus one run-tick each side per tick of flight) stays inside the room's frame (D-058: no launch may carry the cat out of bounds). Hazards inside the envelope are allowed only if a declared betrayal route dies on them (the route validator then checks its lead).
   - `ValidateBand`: geysers only in levels 11+.
   - Timing: a route step that must enter the column during an eruption is a timed step; window ≥ 12 (≥ 8 inside a precision section, D-083).
5. **Trap Lab room 8:** a high exit ledge over a wall no jump can clear. A geyser erupts on a ~2 s rhythm. Betrayals: one launch lands you into ceiling spikes revealed by a chained `HiddenSpikes` (Dies, lead ≥ 6); stepping onto the vent during the tell and then walking off leaves you under the ledge (Recovers). Solution: stand on the vent, ride the eruption, steer onto the ledge.

## 11. Rulings (Phase 1, 2026-09-26)

**Status:** Ruled. Go to Phase 2 once Unity MCP is back (compile, tests and the harness need it). Where these differ from §2–§6 they amend the ticket.

**Accepted as traced:**
- Q1: the push goes in the room step (after the motor, before physics), with the existing hold, pause and reset gates, and uses the previous physics pose like every trap.
- Q2: the `ApplyLaunch` seam: frozen → no-op; keep the cross component and set the component along the direction to `speed`; `coyoteTimer = 0`; `IsGrounded = false`; the jump buffer is untouched. `JumpWindows.cs` doesn't change. A jump pressed on the first push tick is overwritten by the launch; record it in D-088.
- Q4: no speed cap needed; say why in D-088 (the fall cap is along gravity only; 0.28 u/tick < the 0.56 collider height).
- Q5's discrete numbers: the apex test predicts with the **discrete** rise (Σ over ticks, ≈ 3.13 u above the column exit), not the continuous 3.27. Its tolerance is one push tick (0.28 u), because the overshoot past the column top depends on where the cat starts. The horizontal spread uses one run tick (0.12 u) per tick. The ticket's "≈ 3.27 u / ~4.8 u" figures are superseded by what Phase 2 measures.

### R1 · Baseline

The developer's PAX-085 Test Runner total is the baseline. Until it's reported, use **843**, and treat any lower total in Phase 2 as a regression to report.

### R2 · Envelope: counted to the apex, option (a)

Approved. Count the envelope from the first push to the apex (≈ 29 ticks, ≈ 3.5 u each side at one run tick per tick). Past the apex the cat falls like after any jump, and the existing rules (bounds kill D-058, route replays) cover that. The envelope's job is narrower than §2.4 said:
1. **Containment:** the envelope plus the cat's height stays inside the room's frame. No launch may carry the cat out through the top or sides.
2. **Hidden hazards:** a *disguised* hazard (hidden spikes, a disguised arrow, a chained trap) inside the envelope needs a declared betrayal route that dies on it, so its lead is measured (≥ 6). *Honest*, visible hazards inside the envelope are allowed without a route (D-056 (4)). This replaces §2.4's "any hazard needs a betrayal".

### R3 · `SoloRoomBuilder.cs` is on the list

Yes. "Builder" in §6 means `SoloRoomBuilder.cs`; one `case` line, as for the inverter. `RouteReplay.cs` stays off the list: tests read `GeyserTrap` directly. If the harness turns out to need it, stop and ask.

### R4 · Room 8's spikes: their own trigger, not a chain

Approved, with the trigger placed so it passes D-074 as written:
- The ceiling spikes (`HiddenSpikes`) sit over the side of the vent the solution **never** walks or flies through (the far side, away from the start and the exit ledge).
- Their trigger is an ordinary Overlap trigger and a **full-storey cut** on that side (floor to ceiling underside, the storey-aware rule from PAX-059). So `ValidateTriggerCoverage` passes without any exemption or learned-bypass reason. A player who walks under them on the floor sees them pop out of the ceiling. That's harmless and still teaches.
- The Dies betrayal is the launch steered the wrong way. Phase 2 measures its lead. **If the lead is under 6**, or the coverage rule rejects the trigger, stop and report with the numbers; don't move to a chain or a bypass on your own.
- No chain to the geyser: a periodic fire would reveal the spikes on every eruption, including on the solution.

### R5 · Ceiling clearance: approved

`ValidateGeyser`'s "the column overlaps no solid" clause tests the column **plus the cat's collider height (read from `CatMotorConfig`, 0.56 today) above its top**. Otherwise the cat stays in the column and is pushed into the solid every tick of the eruption. A solid inside the ballistic rise beyond that (a bonk) is allowed.

### R6 · Up and Down: approved

Both directions. The direction must match the host face (Floor → Up, Ceiling → Down); a mismatch is a validator error. The room's gravity isn't checked (flip zones make it dynamic); a Down geyser in a gravity-down room just pushes the cat into the floor, which is harmless. Room 8 uses Up; one harness test covers Down.

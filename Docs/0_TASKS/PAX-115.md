# PAX-115 · Flip platforms (D-187 (3))

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-24 (the developer, with PAX-114: "different rotations").
**Phase 1 size:** Lite. This extends the D-119 hinge rotation to a 180° turn about the centre.
**Decisions:** D-091, D-119, D-124, D-187.

## 1. What to build

1. **Flip.** A `MovingTrap` Solid with `flipTicks` > 0 turns 180° about its centre over `flipTicks`, holds `holdTicks`, and
   turns back over `returnTicks`.
   - It fires on its trigger (landing on it, after a delay) or on a Periodic beat.
2. **Spiked face.** Spikes cover its underside and are always shown, so the flip is honest.
   - A cat touching the spiked face, in any pose, dies.
   - So a cat still standing on it when it turns over is impaled, and a cat under it at rest is too.
3. **Snapshot and rewind:** the angle is a function of the ticks since the fire (D-091).
4. **Validator:**
   - `flipTicks` ≥ 6.
   - The disc it turns through overlaps no fixed solid.
   - A repeating flip's cooldown covers flip + hold + back.
5. **Tests:** the angle math, the spiked face kill (on top after the flip, not before), and the validator rules.

## 2. Allowed files

`TrapPath.cs`, `MovingTrap.cs`, `SoloRoomElementTypes.cs` (`MovingFloorSettings` gains `FlipTicks`), `TrapKitSetup.MovingFloors.cs`,
`LevelLayoutValidator.MovingFloors.cs`, `RouteReplay.cs`, new tests, `Docs/07_DECISIONS.md`, this ticket.

## 3. Out of scope

Final art. Turns other than 180°.

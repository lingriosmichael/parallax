## 1. Why

A cloud that hunts you is a different kind of pressure from every other trap: it doesn't wait for a trigger in a fixed place, it forces the player to keep moving. It still has to obey D-040: same input, same outcome, and every strike dodgeable on first sight.

## 2. What to build

1. **Element `StormCloud`** (appended). Authored pose (x, y); horizontal range `MinX..MaxX`; flies at its authored y only. **Dormant** until its trigger fires (Overlap or Chain, Once): while dormant it looks like a harmless cloud (disguise, D-056 (4)). At most one per room.
2. **Follow (pure `StormCloudMath` in Core).** Each room tick while awake and not charging: `x += clamp(catX − x, −FollowSpeed, +FollowSpeed)`, clamped to the range, using the cat's position from the previous tick's post-physics record (the same record routes read). Default `FollowSpeed` **0.08 u/tick** (4 u/s), slower than the cat's 0.12, so the player can outrun it.
3. **Strike cycle,** counted from the wake tick, every `StrikePeriod` ticks (default **100** = 2 s; first strike after `FirstStrikeDelay`, default 50):
   - **Charge (tell):** `TellTicks` (default **25**). The cloud stops, darkens, and draws a faint target line down its locked x. Harmless.
   - **Strike:** `StrikeTicks` (default 6). A lethal vertical column `StrikeWidth` wide (default 0.8) from the cloud's bottom down to the first **static** top below the locked x (a height profile baked at setup from the layout's Floor/Wall/PitBottom elements, D-059 style; no runtime raycasts).
   - Then it resumes following.
   - Kills through `RoomDeath` (`DeathCause.Hazard`) on overlap of the tick pose, like arrows.
4. **Dodge rule (fairness), validated:** `TellTicks ≥ ticksToClear + slack`, where `ticksToClear` is the ticks for a cat **at rest** under the column's centre to move its collider fully out of it (half strike width + half collider width, with the measured acceleration, D-056 (1)), and slack is 12 (8 inside a precision section, D-083). With the defaults: 0.9 u from rest ≈ 10 ticks, so ≥ 22; 25 passes.
5. **Other validator rules:** at most one cloud per room; its whole strike range (every column pose between `MinX` and `MaxX`) keeps door clearance (D-060) from the door, so reaching the door is never a lightning test; its range and y lie inside the frame; `ValidateBand`: levels 11+ only.
6. **Reset:** room reset (D-041) returns it to its authored pose, dormant. Hold and pause freeze it (room ticks).
7. **Routes.** The cloud is a killer kind the harness can name (re-run its column test). A betrayal that dies to a strike reveals by the cloud's charge (lead = strike tick − charge start ≥ 6, trivially 25). The solution must survive every strike it lives through; the route validator proves it by replay.
8. **Trap Lab room 10:** a long room with a raised middle section. Crossing the trigger wakes the cloud; the player has to keep moving and use cover (a ceiling overhang: the strike stops at the first static top, so standing under an overhang is safe). Betrayals: standing still (Dies), waiting under a fake overhang (`FakePlatform`, Dies). Solution through to the door.

## 3. Phase 1 (Full, ≤ 150 lines; at most 6 questions)

1. Which cat position does the cloud read each tick (Transform vs Rigidbody vs the route record), and is it identical in Play and the harness? The follow must not read interpolated Transform positions.
2. Order in the room tick: cloud follow, strike test, other traps. Show it can't differ between Play and the harness.
3. The height profile: which elements count as static tops (Floor, Wall, PitBottom, Ceiling underside for overhangs); do fake platforms and collapsing floors block lightning? (Proposed: no, only static elements; that's what makes a fake overhang a troll.)
4. Gravity-up cats on the ceiling: the strike only goes down from the cloud; confirm a cat above the cloud can't be hit, and say whether the validator should reject a cloud in a room with gravity flips (proposed: allowed).
5. Camera: is the cloud's charge always on screen given it tracks the cat? Show it with D-083 (6)'s rule on room 10.

## 4. Rules and stop conditions

- Follow and strikes are pure functions of room ticks and the recorded cat positions. No `Time.time`, no randomness (D-067), no physics queries.
- If the follow can read a different position in Play than in the harness, stop and report.

## 5. Tests (seen red first)

- `StormCloudMath`: follow step and clamp, dormant/awake, charge lock, strike window, cycle timing.
- Height profile: strike length over floor, pit and overhang.
- Harness: a cat standing still dies on the first strike; a cat that starts moving at the charge tick survives; an overhang blocks the strike.
- Dodge rule, door-clearance, one-per-room, `ValidateBand` clauses. Reset/hold/pause. Room 10 routes and the camera tell rule. Two replays identical (D-079 (3)).

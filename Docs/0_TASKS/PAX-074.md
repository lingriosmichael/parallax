## 1. Why

Arrows are the kit's first projectile, and they're the most common trap in the genre after
spikes and fake floors. KIT-3 (troll routes) and KIT-4 (precision sections, D-069 (2)(b)) both
plan to mix arrows in. The kit has moving hazards (D-055 (3)) but nothing that:
- launches from a wall along a lane;
- has a tell that is harmless while visible;
- stops at a wall;
- can be read by the validator as a lane rather than a box.

## 2. What to build

1. **An arrow trap.** A launcher fires one arrow along a horizontal lane, left→right or
   right→left.
   - Each fire has three phases:
     1. **Tell:** `TellTicks`, visible and harmless.
     2. **Flight:** constant speed, in whole u per tick.
     3. **Stop:** the arrow halts at the lane's authored end, which is a wall or Solid face. From
        then on it's harmless and stays visible until the trap is reset or re-armed.
   - The arrow kills on overlap through `RoomDeath`. The test is against its pose computed for
     that tick (D-055 (3)), not physics.
   - The arrow's motion is a pure function of ticks since the fire (D-051, D-055).
   - There are no physics raycasts, and flight never ends early by hitting something.
2. **Trigger sources and repeat modes from the existing kit:** Overlap, Chain and Periodic, with
   Once, Rearm and Periodic (D-055). Nothing new at runtime.
   - **"Jump-triggered" is layout only:** an Overlap trigger placed in the jump arc, so only a
     jumping cat enters it. No new trigger source and no motor event.
   - Phase 1 §3.5 says how D-074 coverage treats a trigger like that.
3. **Disguise (D-056 (4)).** A launcher is either honest (visible) or disguised (wall look).
   Before it fires, a disguised launcher looks like the wall it sits in. The tell must be visible
   in both cases.
4. **Reset (D-055 (4)).** Room reset restores the arrow to its launcher, un-fired and unseen,
   along with its arming, chain state and repeat counters. The death hold (D-059) and pause
   (D-073) freeze arrows through the existing gates, with nothing new.
5. **Validator support.** Each rule is a separately named method, never `Validate` (see §3.6):
   - the tell is at least D-057's lead;
   - no tunnelling;
   - the lane has a clear path;
   - door clearance for the lane;
   - coverage for arrow triggers;
   - timing slack where an arrow is a survive case.
6. **One dev room in `Sandbox_TrapLab`** so the developer can play every variant. There's no
   change to L001–L004 or `SoloRoomsLayout`.

## 4. Rules

- Deterministic: no `Time.time`, no randomness, no physics queries for flight. Timing is in whole
  ticks through `TickTime` where seconds appear (D-075).
- No change to L001–L004, `SoloRoomsLayout`, `Level_Solo01`, or any existing trap's behaviour.
- No change to thresholds (12, 6, 30, 0.12 u) or to the tick rate.
- No new Unity package, no hand-edited `.unity`/`.prefab`/`.asset`, no `ProjectSettings/`.
- Tunables go in a config asset under `Assets/_Game/Data`, only if Phase 1 shows layout data
  isn't enough. If one is needed, it's created by a setup menu.
- Changing an existing test needs my approval by name. New validator rules go in separately
  named methods, never in or named `Validate`.

## 5. Tests (EditMode)

1. **Arrow motion (pure):**
   - pose per tick for both directions;
   - harmless for exactly `TellTicks`;
   - stops at the lane end and is harmless afterwards;
   - the fire tick is counted per D-055 for Overlap, Chain and Periodic (room start plus phase).
2. **No tunnelling:** at the proposed cap, an arrow and a cat moving towards each other at their
   fastest can't pass without an overlap tick. The first speed above the cap is rejected by the
   validator.
3. **Kill and survive, runtime rig:**
   - a lane at standing height kills a standing cat;
   - the same lane misses a cat whose collider is fully above it;
   - the death goes through `RoomDeath`, and the hold freezes the arrow.
4. **Reset:** Once, Rearm and Periodic each restore their authored state on a room reset. Rearm
   snaps back at the end of its cooldown.
5. **Validator:** a fixture that fails and one that passes for each §2.5 rule, including the
   §3.5 coverage outcome. Each fixture is seen red.
6. **Existing validator rules over `LevelLayouts`:** unchanged results for L001–L004. Existing
   tests cover this; name them.
7. Every existing test stays green. Total = 484 + new tests.

Mark each new test "seen red" (quote the failure) or "unproven" in the handover.

- **R1. Folder.** `ArrowTrap.cs` goes in `Assets/_Game/Gameplay/Rooms/`, `ArrowMath.cs` in

# PAX-037 — Gate 3 closure and device verification session

**Type:** developer session. No Claude Code implementation step.
**Owner:** developer (device), Architect (review of findings).
**Blocks:** PAX-038 (tilt), PAX-028 onward (networking).
**Depends on:** PAX-036 committed, PAX-V01 committed, Editor acceptance 1–14 passed.

---

## Why this ticket now

Gate 3 passed in the Editor only. Phases 4 and 5 (anchors, transport, Echo, plate/gate, control
station) have never run on hardware, and three tickets deferred touch tests. Every further ticket
either needs a phone (tilt, Gate 4) or adds a layer on top of unverified device state (networking).
If a touch or latency bug is found after Photon lands, it will be entangled with Fusion.

PAX-V01 is what makes this session feasible: the sandbox is now readable on a small screen.

## Test plan

Each test records: pass / fail / blocked, the logcat error and warning counts during it, and a
screenshot. Measured values go in the table at the end.

### T1 — Boot and baseline
Launch, do nothing for 30 s, then move each cat a few steps.
**Pass:** zero errors in logcat. Both realities render. Labels are legible at arm's length and
follow their objects. No missing-script warnings.

### T2 — Debug overlay layout
The panel is now ~420 px with the Checkpoint row, and LBL was added at `(90, 45, 70, 30)`.
**Pass:** no row is clipped at the bottom of the panel. DBG, LBL and the touch-input overlay do
not overlap each other. Neither button sits inside a PAX-017 reserved touch region or inside the
stick / jump / interact zones. Every one is hittable first try.

### T3 — Movement and the open analog question
Walk, jump, land, fall, all four gravity directions. Then specifically: half-push vs full-push of
the stick, and a 45° push.
**Pass:** movement is responsive and cat-relative (D-021).
**Record:** is the half vs full speed difference perceptible? Does a 45° push (~0.71 on a single
axis) read as a sluggish cat? These are the Gate 2 open questions and this is where they close or
become a tuning ticket.

### T4 — Interact rect (deferred from PAX-021)
Approach the vine in A and the station in A, and trigger both by touch only.
**Pass:** the interact zone `(0.56, 0.00, 0.18, 0.40)` can be hit without looking down, and does
not fire while walking past. **Record** a better rect if it needs one.

### T5 — REC button and Echo (deferred from PAX-023)
Latency 300 ms. RECORD by touch, walk ~5 s, SWITCH, watch the Echo replay.
**Pass:** the REC button is hittable, the HUD bar shows progress then `HOLD`, the Echo replays the
recorded path, and switching back resumes the human from the Echo's final pose.

### T6 — Plate and gate, Type B solve (Gate 3 core)
Latency 300 ms. A records itself standing on the plate, switches; A's Echo holds the gate open
while B walks through to the goal.
**Pass:** solves on device. The gate opens after the transport delay, not instantly.
Also confirm: switching away while standing on the plate does **not** hold the gate (D-028).

### T7 — Dial with touch, and the sideways-cat concern (deferred from PAX-026)
Latency 300 ms. Sit A at the station, drag the dial through a full sweep.
**Pass:** the dial tracks the finger; **Cat B's** gravity follows after the delay and **Cat A's**
does not. Standing up hides the dial and leaves B's gravity as it was.
This is the test that settles the screenshot where Cat A stood on the left wall. If A's gravity
changes at any point here, stop and report.

### T8 — PAX-036 on device
Run acceptance steps 1–14 from `Docs/TASKS/PAX-036.md`. Priority order if time is short:
the Respawn button, checkpoint advance and no-regress, fall-volume respawn, Echo never fall-reset,
respawn with no checkpoint reached (spawn Y −3.10), step 11 (respawn while seated), step 12
(respawn mid-Echo), step 14 (D-032: dial at 90°, B falls, B returns already at 90°).

### T9 — Backgrounding and resume
Home button mid-recording, wait 10 s, return. Then again mid-Echo, and again while seated at the
station.
**Pass:** no errors, no phantom input, no stuck latch, Echo and seat state are coherent on return.

### T10 — Gate 3 session
Five continuous minutes of solo play across both realities, mirroring the Gate 2 format: move,
jump, fall, respawn, switch, record, Echo, plate, gate, elevator, station, goal.
**Pass:** zero errors in logcat for the full five minutes. **This is the Gate 3 verdict.**

## Stop conditions

Stop the session and report if any of these appear:
- Any repeating error in logcat.
- A control that cannot be hit reliably (report the rect, do not tune it live — Play-mode changes
  are lost).
- Cat A's gravity changing during T7.
- An Echo that does not replay, or replays at the wrong position.

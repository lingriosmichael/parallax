# PAX-116 · Spinning hazards (D-187 (4))

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-24 (the developer, with PAX-114: "spinning hazards").
**Phase 1 size:** Lite. This is a new element kind with its own small runtime, on the existing `RoomTrap` timing.
**Decisions:** D-040 (deterministic), D-091, D-187.

## 1. What to build

1. **`SoloRoomElementKind.Spinner`** (appended). The element's position is the pivot.
   - 1–4 spiked arms of `armLength` × `armWidth` turn at `degreesPerTick` (sign = direction) from `startAngle`.
   - It turns either from the room's first tick (`alwaysOn`) or from its fire (overlap or chain trigger).
   - The angle is a function of ticks, so a reset and a rewind restore it exactly.
2. **Runtime:** `SpinnerTrap : RoomTrap`. Each tick it turns its arms and kills a cat overlapping any arm (an oriented box test).
   The hub is harmless.
3. **Builder:** `SpinnerSettings`, with placeholder art: red arms and a grey hub.
4. **Validator:**
   - 1–4 arms, a positive length, width 0.2–0.6.
   - A tip speed of at most 0.5 u a tick.
   - The disc stays inside the room and overlaps no fixed solid (touching is allowed).
   - It doesn't cover the door or a checkpoint.
   - Its kill volume is the disc's bounding box.
5. **Tests:** angle math, the arm kill test, the validator rules, and snapshot restore.

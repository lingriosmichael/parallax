# PAX-129 · Cat skills: the air jump and the whisker dodge

**Status:** Approved by the developer 2026-11-05 ("implement them both", "On everywhere", "a cooldown but every 5 seconds"). Decision D-324.
**Phase 1 size: Full** (a motor change); settled in conversation, recorded in D-324.

## 1. Rules

- **Amendment 2 (D-324):** the air jump is off (`airJumps` 0) after the developer's play-test; the rules below stay in code.

- Air jump: one, 1 u, in the air after coyote; back on landing, a wall or a vine; a press about to land is the buffered jump;
  the press stays buffered for a wall jump or a vine leap; a geyser launch spends it.
- Whisker dodge: 0.3 s through arrows and lasers; the next press 5 s later; a respawn makes it ready.

# PAX-A10 · App icon and store graphics

**Status:** Draft. Starts after PAX-A06 and PAX-A09.
**Depends on:** PAX-A06, PAX-A09

## Scope

1. **App icon:** adaptive icon (foreground + background layers) and the 512 × 512 Play Store
   icon; legible at 48 px; the cat, not text.
2. **Feature graphic** 1024 × 500.
3. **Screenshots:** landscape captures from the Editor at the phone's resolution, from real
   levels with final art (at least 4; check Play's current count and size limits at the time).
4. Optional short promo video: out of scope for v1 unless cheap.

Check Google Play's current asset specs when starting; they change. Icon import settings go
through a setup/Player Settings step the developer does by hand (ProjectSettings is off-limits
to agents).

## Acceptance

Icon checked on a launcher mock at small size; graphics match Play specs exactly.
